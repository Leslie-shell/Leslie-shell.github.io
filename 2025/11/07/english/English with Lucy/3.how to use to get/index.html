2 how to use 'to get'

发表于 2025-11-07 更新于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， StudyWithLucy 阅读次数：
本文字数： 1.6k 阅读时长 ≈ 1 分钟

一、动词 “get” 的多种含义

Lucy 说明 “get” 在英语中非常常用，有超过 50种 不同用法。
她将其归纳为 6类核心意思：

表示 到达（to arrive / to travel）

“get” 可以替代 “arrive” 或 “travel”。

  • How did you get to London?
    = How did you travel to London?
    （你怎么去伦敦的？）
  • What time did you get here?
    = What time did you arrive here?
    （你几点到的？）

表示 获得 / 收到（to receive / to obtain）

“get” 可表示“得到”“收到”。

  • I got a letter in the post. = I received a letter.
    我收到了信。
  • I hope I get a promotion. = I hope I receive a promotion.
    我希望升职。
  • He’s not going to get an apology.
    他不会得到道歉。
  • I get presents at Christmas.
    我圣诞节会收到礼物。
  • Can I get a kiss?
    我能得到一个吻吗？

表示 变得（to become）

“get + 形容词 / 比较级 / 过去分词” 表示“变成……”。

  • In summer it gets hot.
    夏天变热。
  • I hope I get better at English.
    我希望我的英语越来越好。
  • I’m getting bored.
    我开始觉得无聊。

表示 拿 / 买 / 找（to bring / to buy / to find）

根据语境不同，意思会变化。

  • Can I get you a drink?
    在酒吧 = 我请你喝一杯。
    在家 = 我去拿饮料给你。
  • I need to get some new clothes.
    我要去买新衣服。
  • Were you able to get the information?
    你找到资料了吗？

与 have / have to 连用（意义不变）

“get” 可以与 “have” 或 “have to” 互换，语义相同。

① 表示“拥有” (have / have got)

  • I have so much money. = I’ve got so much money.
  • I don’t have any friends. = I’ve not got any friends.
  • Do you have enough time? = Have you got enough time?

② 表示“必须” (have to / have got to)

  • I have to tell her. = I’ve got to tell her.
  • She doesn’t have to come. = She hasn’t got to come.
  • Do you have to leave? = Have you got to leave?

常见固定表达（Expressions with “get”）

这些都是地道口语中的高频表达。

  • Do you get it? → 你明白吗？
  • Did you get it right? → 你答对了吗？
  • I need to get going. → 我该走了。
  • We didn’t get off to a good start. → 我们开局不太好。
  • You’re getting on my nerves. → 你让我心烦。

二、总结记忆法

含义 对应动词 例句
到达 arrive I got here at 10.
收到 receive I got a letter.
变得 become It gets cold at night.
买 / 拿 / 找 buy / bring / find Can I get you a drink?
拥有 have I’ve got two cats.
必须 have to I’ve got to go now.
表达 习语 Do you get it?

如果你只想记一个核心思路：

“GET = 到达 + 获得 + 变化 + 拿/买 + 拥有 + 必须 + 习语”
