词根词汇学习文档 – Gress & Habit

1. 词根：Gress (走，行进)

Root: gress /ɡres/ → “走、前进、行进”

单词 音标 词性 中文释义 英文解释 例句
aggress /əˈɡres/ v./adj. 侵略；进攻；好斗的 to attack or behave aggressively The army will not aggress without reason.
congress /ˈkɒŋɡres/ n. 国会；代表大会 a formal meeting of representatives The US Congress passed the new law.
digress /daɪˈɡres/ v. 离题 to go off the main subject He often digresses when telling a story.
egress /ˈiːɡres/ n. 离开；出口 the act of going out; an exit Emergency egress must be kept clear.
ingress /ˈɪnɡres/ n. 入口 the act of entering Ingress to the building is via the main gate.
progress /ˈprəʊɡres/ n./v. 进展；前进 forward movement or development The project is making good progress.
regress /rɪˈɡres/ v./n. 回归；退步 to return to a previous state After the illness, he regressed in his skills.
transgress /trænˈɡres/ v. 越界；违法 to go beyond limits; violate He transgressed the school rules.

短语 / 记忆技巧：

  • gress = go → 所有单词都与“走、前进或越界”相关
  • progress / regress → 进步 / 退步
  • ingress / egress → 入口 / 出口
  • digress → 离题 → 从正路“走”偏

2. 词根：Habit (居住 / 习惯)

Root: habit /ˈhæbɪt/ → “居住；习惯”

单词 音标 词性 中文释义 英文解释 例句
cohabit /kəʊˈhæbɪt/ v. 同居 to live together, often as a couple They decided to cohabit before marriage.
habitable /ˈhæbɪtəbl/ adj. 适合居住的 suitable for living in The house is habitable despite its age.
habitant /ˈhæbɪtənt/ n. 居民；居住者 a person who lives in a place The habitants of the village are very friendly.
habitat /ˈhæbɪtæt/ n. 栖息地 the natural home of an animal or plant The panda’s natural habitat is bamboo forests.

短语 / 记忆技巧：

  • habit = live / dwell / habit
  • cohabit → together + live → 同居
  • habitable → 可居住的 → 环境可适应
  • habitant → 居住者 → 人
  • habitat → 栖息地 → 地点

3. 词根总结与记忆法
