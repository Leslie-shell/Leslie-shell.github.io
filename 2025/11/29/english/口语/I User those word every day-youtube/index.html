I use those words every day

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， 口语 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.3k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟

I use those words every day (www.youtube.com...)

  1. on a roll

    • It’s best to do liaison (/liˈeɪzɒn/); otherwise, it’s a bit tongue-twisting

    • This lovely phrase on a roll means that you are on a streak of success. 一连串的成功

    • I have been productive all morning, I got everything done on my to-do list, I can’t believe it. I didn’t get sidetracked, I didn’t procrastinate, I’m on a roll. If you like to watch sports, you can also use this phrase, you might say, “They scored three goals in a row. They are on a roll!” Yes.

  2. ahead of time

    • earlier than expected or required
    • eg:I finished my report ahead of time so I could relax

  3. get out of hand.

    • What if I said, “during the week, dishes and laundry sometimes get out of control because we’re so busy.” Well, you can get the idea that there are dishes in the sink, dishes on the counter. There’s laundry in the basket, maybe there’s laundry even in the washer that hasn’t been dried yet.
    • There’s a little bit of chaos, because things get busy during the week. But we can use this great phrase instead, at least boost your vocabulary while you’re talking about chaos, you could say, “Dishes and laundry get out of hand during the week.” We can also use this to talk about people or places where people are involved. So take a look at this.
    • eg:I don’t want this task to get out of hand.

  4. it’s too bad.

    • It’s too bad we don’t have a maid who could help to clean up our house.
    • it’s too bad that we elected someone else who is incompetent.
    • (I suggest It’s best not to use it. Sometimes it’s very difficult for us to distinguish between scenarios)

  5. when the time comes for.

    • I’m learning English, so when the time comes for me to go to the US for vacation, I am going to be prepared.
    • When the time comes for Matilda, my baby to go to preschool, our house is going to be very quiet.

  6. get some fresh air

    • Do you know what this means? It just means going outside.

  7. come to think of it

    • Come to think of it, it is snack time. Let’s go eat something! 仔细想想，想来

  8. to be spoiled 被宠坏

    • This is a way to truly show love, I think. So for me, I have several neighbors that are constantly sharing food together. I feel spoiled. I am spoiled that my neighbors share such good food with me. What do you think this means, to be spoiled? This just means that I am treated well. Maybe even above well. I am pampered. Someone is taking care of me more than is even necessary.
    • Your children are spoiled. We do sometimes use this negatively for children.

  9. the fact that

    • The fact that he is late makes me angry.

  10. accommodate

    1. The hotel can accommodate up to 500 guests.（这家酒店最多可容纳500名客人。）

      We will try to accommodate your special needs.（我们会尽力满足你的特殊需求。）

      You should be able to accommodate your lifestyle to the new changes.（你应该能够使自己的生活方式适应这些新变化。 ）

    • be/get sidetracked

  2. procrastinate

    1. 拖延
