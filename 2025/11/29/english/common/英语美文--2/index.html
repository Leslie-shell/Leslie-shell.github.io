Childlike Happiness

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Article 阅读次数：
本文字数： 5.6k 阅读时长 ≈ 5 分钟

In each of our childhood years, happiness seemed to be an innate instinct, coming naturally without any need for technique. As children, we appeared to find joy in the most ordinary things — a butterfly dancing in the air, a playful splash in a puddle, or a game of hide-and-seek. Back then, our minds were undistracted, unbothered by worldly affairs, and laughter flowed as water from a spring.

Yet, as we grow older, we gradually feel the rush and pressure of this world. Streets are bustling, traffic flows endlessly, and people always seem to be in a hurry, chasing their individual goals, their faces often marked with worry or fatigue. In this whirlpool of complex responsibilities and expectations, happiness seems to have become a skill that requires deliberate practice. In a process where everyone appears to be striving for achievements, wealth, and recognition, we must learn to actively seek and create happiness amidst life’s heavy burdens.可是，随着年岁增长，我们逐渐感受到这个世界的匆忙和压力。街头巷尾，车水马龙，人们总是行色匆匆，奔波于各自的目标，脸上常挂着忧虑或疲惫。在这纷繁的责任和期望的漩涡中，快乐似乎成了一门需要刻意修炼的技艺。在每个人似乎都在为成就、财富、认可奔波的过程里，我们必须要学会在生活的重担中主动寻找和创造快乐。

Maintaining a childlike spirit doesn’t mean evading adult responsibilities; instead, it’s about facing people and situations with a composed mindset. Embracing our inner child means rediscovering the essence of play. We learn to find surprises in the everyday — the warmth of the first ray of morning sun, a flower swaying in the wind in our yard, or a weekend gathering with friends. We can also immerse ourselves wholeheartedly in certain activities without worrying about speed or gains and losses — it could be photography, gardening, karaoke, or simply curling up on the sofa to watch a movie.

On this journey of growth, may we always be surrounded by love. At the same time, permit yourself to be who you truly are. Being authentic doesn’t mean seeking flawlessness. So in this world where everyone is in a rush, let’s make a pact with our inner child. We should cherish its wisdom, learn its skill of finding joy in the present, and its courage to be our authentic selves. Even as we shoulder adult responsibilities, we must always carry this love, happiness, and that evergreen beauty of childlike innocence, wherever we go.

1.词汇解析

Vocabulary Summary｜词汇整理

1. innate instinct — n.

Pronunciation: /ɪˈneɪt ˈɪnstɪŋkt/
中文: 天生的本能
Definition: natural ability or tendency you are born with
Example: Happiness seemed to be an innate instinct in our childhood.
童年时，幸福似乎是一种天生的本能。

2. playful splash — n.

Pronunciation: /ˈpleɪfəl splæʃ/
中文: 顽皮的水花
Definition: a fun, light water activity
Example: A playful splash in a puddle brought us laughter.
水坑里的一次顽皮水花带给我们欢笑。

3. undistracted — adj.

Pronunciation: /ʌn dɪˈstræktɪd/
中文: 不被分心的
Definition: not having attention diverted
Example: As children, our minds were undistracted.
童年时，我们的心智专注而不被分心。

4. fatigue — n.

Pronunciation: /fəˈtiːɡ/
中文: 疲惫
Definition: extreme tiredness
Example: Faces often marked with worry or fatigue.
脸上常常带着忧虑或疲惫的痕迹。

5. rush and pressure — n.

Pronunciation: /rʌʃ ənd ˈprɛʃər/
中文: 匆忙与压力
Definition: hurry and stress
Example: We gradually feel the rush and pressure of this world.
我们逐渐感受到这个世界的匆忙与压力。

6. composed mindset — n.

Pronunciation: /kəmˈpoʊzd ˈmaɪndset/
中文: 冷静的心态
Definition: calm and controlled mental state
Example: Facing situations with a composed mindset.
以冷静的心态应对各种情况。

7. rediscover — v.

Pronunciation: /ˌriːdɪsˈkʌvər/
中文: 重新发现
Definition: find again
Example: Rediscovering the essence of play.
重新发现游戏的本质。

8. immerse oneself — v.

Pronunciation: /ɪˈmɜːrs wʌnˈsɛlf/
中文: 全身心投入
Definition: involve completely
Example: Immerse ourselves in activities like photography or gardening.
全身心投入到摄影或园艺等活动中。

9. authentic — adj.

Pronunciation: /ɔːˈθɛntɪk/
中文: 真诚的；真实的
Definition: genuine, true to oneself
Example: Permit yourself to be who you truly are.
允许自己成为真正的自己。

10. evergreen beauty — n.

Pronunciation: /ˈɛvərɡriːn ˈbjuːti/
中文: 常青的美丽
Definition: enduring charm, timeless purity
Example: Carry the evergreen beauty of childlike innocence.
保持孩童般纯真的常青美丽。

2. 句子分析

句子 1

In each of our childhood years, happiness seemed to be an innate instinct, coming naturally without any need for technique.

分析：

  • 主句：happiness seemed to be an innate instinct
  • 分词短语修饰：coming naturally without any need for technique → 描述 happiness 的特质
  • 修辞：强调童年时快乐是自然天成的，无需努力

句子 2

As children, we appeared to find joy in the most ordinary things — a butterfly dancing in the air, a playful splash in a puddle, or a game of hide-and-seek.

分析：

  • 主句：we appeared to find joy in the most ordinary things
  • 列举法（em dash）：三种童年乐趣 → 生动描写童年画面
  • 技巧：使用动词 + 名词短语 → 形成拟人化画面

句子 3

Yet, as we grow older, we gradually feel the rush and pressure of this world. Streets are bustling, traffic flows endlessly, and people always seem to be in a hurry, chasing their individual goals, their faces often marked with worry or fatigue.

分析：

  • 复合句 + 并列句
  • rush and pressure → 核心名词短语，强调生活压力
  • bustling / flows endlessly → 拟人化/动态动词
  • 写作技巧：节奏快，形成对比 → 与童年平静的快乐形成反差

句子 4

Maintaining a childlike spirit doesn’t mean evading adult responsibilities; instead, it’s about facing people and situations with a composed mindset.

分析：

  • 对比句结构：doesn’t mean …; instead …
  • composed mindset → 核心短语，表达心理状态
  • 逻辑：强调“童心”并非逃避，而是以冷静心态面对生活

句子 5

On this journey of growth, may we always be surrounded by love. At the same time, permit yourself to be who you truly are.

分析：

  • 愿望句 + 祈使句
  • 写作技巧：短句清晰，强调情感 → 用于劝导 / 教育性文章

句子 6

Being authentic doesn’t mean seeking flawlessness. So in this world where everyone is in a rush, let’s make a pact with our inner child.

分析：

  • 对比句 + 主张句
  • inner child → 核心概念
  • pact → 与“童心”建立约定
  • 逻辑：强调接受自我、不追求完美

句子 7

We should cherish its wisdom, learn its skill of finding joy in the present, and its courage to be our authentic selves. Even as we shoulder adult responsibilities, we must always carry this love, happiness, and that evergreen beauty of childlike innocence, wherever we go.

分析：

  • 并列句 + 列举法 → wisdom, skill, courage
  • “Even as …, we must always carry …” → 强调在成长过程中保持童心
  • evergreen beauty → 拟人化，强调童心的持久魅力
