1. remediation /ˌriːmiːdiˈeɪʃn/

n — the act of correcting or improving something; making amends or repairs
中文释义：补救；矫正；修复（常出现在教育和环境等领域）

Examples:

  • The school provides remediation for students who are struggling with math.
  • Soil remediation is necessary to clean up the polluted area.

Common Phrases:

  • academic remediation — 学术补习
  • environmental remediation — 环境修复

Synonyms:

  • correction
  • repair
  • improvement

Word Root:

  • Root: remedi- (Latin: “to cure, to remedy”) → 与补救、矫正相关
  • Suffix: -ation 表示名词
  • Related Words:
    • remediate — 修复；补救
    • remedial — 补救的；矫正的

2. ordinary /ˈɔːrdəneri/

adj — not unusual or different in any way
中文释义：普通的，一般的

Examples:

  • an ordinary sort of day — 平平常常的一天
  • in the ordinary course of events — 在一般情况下
  • ordinary people like you and me — 像你我这等普通人
  • This was no ordinary meeting. — 这次会议非同寻常

Common Phrases:

  • ordinary life — 普通生活
  • ordinary circumstances — 普通情况

Synonyms:

  • common
  • usual
  • typical

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 ordinarius → regular, customary → 普通的

3. extraordinary /ɪkˈstrɔːrdəneri/

adj — 1. unexpected, surprising, or strange; 2. not normal; greater or better than usual
中文释义：意想不到的；令人惊奇的；奇怪的；非凡的；卓越的

Examples:

  • It’s extraordinary that he managed to sleep through the party. — 真想不到他竟然从聚会开始一直睡到结束
  • What an extraordinary thing to say! — 真是咄咄怪事
  • an extraordinary achievement — 卓越的成就
  • She was a truly extraordinary woman. — 她是位非常杰出的女性
  • They went to extraordinary lengths to explain their behaviour. — 他们竭力为自己的行为辩解

Common Phrases:

  • extraordinary event — 非凡事件
  • extraordinary ability — 卓越能力

Synonyms:

  • exceptional
  • remarkable
  • unusual

Word Root:

  • Root: extra- (Latin: beyond) + ordinary → 超越普通 → 非凡

4. fraction /ˈfrækʃn/

n — 1. a numerical part of a whole (数学：分数)；2. a small part or fragment (小部分；碎片)
中文释义：分数；小部分；碎片

Examples:

  • 1/2 is a proper fraction, while 5/3 is an improper fraction. — 1/2是真分数，而5/3是假分数
  • She struggled to convert the fraction to a decimal. — 她努力把分数转换成小数
  • Only a tiny fraction of solar energy reaches the Earth’s surface. — 只有极小部分的太阳能到达地球表面
  • The ancient vase was shattered into fractions. — 这个古董花瓶摔成了碎片

Common Phrases:

  • proper fraction — 真分数
  • improper fraction — 假分数

Synonyms:

  • portion
  • segment
  • part

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 fractus → broken → 分裂、部分

5. commission /kəˈmɪʃn/

n — 1. committee or group assigned to a task; 2. payment for services (commission); 3. formal request or assignment; 4. military appointment
v — to formally assign someone to a task or grant an appointment
中文释义：委员会；佣金；正式委托；任职；委任

Examples:

  • The government set up a commission to investigate the corruption scandal. — 政府成立了一个委员会来调查腐败丑闻
  • She earns a 10% commission on every car she sells. — 她每卖出一辆车就能赚取10%的佣金
  • The artist received a commission to paint a portrait of the president. — 这位艺术家受委托为总统画一幅肖像
  • He received his commission as a lieutenant in the army. — 他获得了陆军中尉的委任状
  • The company commissioned a study on consumer behavior. — 该公司委托进行了一项关于消费者行为的研究
  • She was commissioned as a captain in the navy last year. — 她去年被正式任命为海军上尉

Common Phrases:

  • sales commission — 销售佣金
  • government commission — 政府委员会
  • commissioned artwork — 受委托的作品

Synonyms:

  • assign
  • authorize
  • delegate

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 committere → entrust → 委托
