1. preface /ˈprefəs/

n — an introduction to a book, typically stating its subject, scope, or aims
中文释义: 序言；开端

Examples:

  • He wrote the preface to the book.
  • The preface explains the author’s motivation for writing.

Common Phrases:

  • book preface — 书序
  • preface remarks — 序言致辞

Synonyms:

  • introduction
  • foreword

2. premise /ˈpremɪs/

n — a statement or idea that is assumed to be true and from which a conclusion is drawn
中文释义: 前提；假设

Examples:

  • The argument is based on a false premise.
  • A good conclusion follows logically from the premise.

Common Phrases:

  • basic premise — 基本前提
  • false premise — 错误假设

Synonyms:

  • assumption
  • proposition
  • basis

3. fragile /ˈfrædʒaɪl/

adj — easily broken or damaged
中文释义: 易碎的；脆弱的

Examples:

  • The vase is fragile, handle with care.
  • Fragile ecosystems need protection.

Common Phrases:

  • fragile balance — 脆弱平衡
  • fragile health — 健康脆弱

Synonyms:

  • delicate
  • breakable
  • vulnerable

4. frustration /frʌˈstreɪʃn/

n — the feeling of being upset or annoyed because of inability to change or achieve something
中文释义: 挫折；沮丧

Examples:

  • She felt frustration at her slow progress.
  • The delays caused considerable frustration.

Common Phrases:

  • deep frustration — 深深的挫折
  • frustration with — 对……的沮丧

Synonyms:

  • annoyance
  • disappointment
  • exasperation

5. significantly /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkəntli/

adv — in a sufficiently great or important way as to be worthy of attention
中文释义: 显著地

Examples:

  • Sales have increased significantly this year.
  • The new policy significantly improved efficiency.

Common Phrases:

  • increase significantly — 显著增长
  • significantly better — 明显更好

Synonyms:

  • considerably
  • substantially
  • markedly

6. impediment /ɪmˈpedɪmənt/

n — a hindrance or obstruction in doing something
中文释义: 障碍；阻碍

Examples:

  • Lack of funds is a major impediment to research.
  • Poor communication can be an impediment to teamwork.

Common Phrases:

  • speech impediment — 言语障碍
  • impediment to progress — 进步障碍

Synonyms:

  • obstacle
  • barrier
  • hindrance

7. sprout /spraʊt/

v — to begin to grow; n — a new shoot or growth from a plant
中文释义: 发芽；嫩芽

Examples:

  • The seeds sprouted within a week.
  • Young sprouts are rich in nutrients.

Common Phrases:

  • sprout leaves — 发芽叶子
  • sprout quickly — 快速生长

Synonyms:

  • germinate
  • shoot
  • bud

8. seedling /ˈsiːdlɪŋ/

n — a young plant grown from a seed
中文释义: 幼苗

Examples:

  • The seedling was planted in the garden.
  • Protect the seedlings from frost.

Common Phrases:

  • young seedling — 幼苗
  • plant seedling — 种植幼苗

Synonyms:

  • sapling
  • young plant

9. seed /siːd/

n — the grain or ripened ovule of a plant from which a new plant can grow
中文释义: 种子

Examples:

  • The farmer planted the seeds in spring.
  • Save seeds for next year’s crop.

Common Phrases:

  • plant seeds — 种植种子
  • seed germination — 种子发芽

Synonyms:

  • kernel
  • grain

10. cone /koʊn/

n — a conical fruit or structure of a conifer tree
中文释义: 圆锥形果；球果

Examples:

  • Pine cones fell from the trees.
  • The cone contains seeds for reproduction.

Common Phrases:

  • pine cone — 松果
  • seed cone — 种子球果

Synonyms:

  • pine fruit
  • fir cone

11. priority /praɪˈɒrəti/

n — the fact or condition of being regarded or treated as more important
中文释义: 优先；优先权

Examples:

  • Safety is our top priority.
  • Give priority to urgent tasks.

Common Phrases:

  • top priority — 最重要的事情
  • high priority — 高优先级

Synonyms:

  • precedence
  • importance
  • preference

12. recruit /rɪˈkruːt/

v — to enlist or hire new members or employees
中文释义: 招募；征募

Examples:

  • The company is recruiting new staff.
  • They recruit volunteers for the project.

Common Phrases:

  • recruit staff — 招聘员工
  • recruit participants — 招募参与者

Synonyms:

  • enlist
  • hire
  • enroll

13. thrill /θrɪl/

n — a sudden feeling of excitement and pleasure; v — to cause excitement
中文释义: 激动；兴奋

Examples:

  • Riding a roller coaster gives me a thrill.
  • The news thrilled the audience.

Common Phrases:

  • thrill of victory — 胜利的喜悦
  • thrilling experience — 激动人心的经历

Synonyms:

  • excitement
  • exhilaration
  • delight
