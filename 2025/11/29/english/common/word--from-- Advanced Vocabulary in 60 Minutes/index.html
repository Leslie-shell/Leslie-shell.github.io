Advanced Vocabulary in 60 Minutes-1

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Vocabulary 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.1k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟
preposterous

1. preposterous /prɪˈpɑːstərəs/

adj — completely unreasonable or ridiculous; contrary to common sense
中文释义：荒谬的；反常的。

Examples:

  • The idea that the earth is flat is preposterous.
  • His excuse was so preposterous that no one believed him.

Common Phrases:

  • preposterous claim — 荒谬的说法
  • preposterous behavior — 荒唐行为

Synonyms:

  • absurd
  • ridiculous
  • ludicrous

Word Root:

  • pre-（在前） + posterus（在后） → “前后颠倒” → 荒谬

2. perfunctory /pərˈfʌŋktəri/

adj — done quickly and without real interest, effort, or care
中文释义：敷衍的；马虎的。

Examples:

  • He gave a perfunctory nod and walked away.
  • Her apology was perfunctory and insincere.

Common Phrases:

  • perfunctory response — 敷衍回应
  • perfunctory greeting — 敷衍的问候

Synonyms:

  • superficial
  • cursory
  • indifferent

3. truculent /ˈtruːkjələnt/

adj — eager to fight; aggressively defiant or hostile
中文释义：好斗的；粗暴的；凶狠的。

Examples:

  • His truculent attitude scared everyone.
  • The animal became truculent when threatened.

Common Phrases:

  • truculent behavior — 暴躁行为
  • truculent speech — 充满攻击性的言辞

Synonyms:

  • aggressive
  • hostile
  • combative

4. austere /ɔːˈstɪr/

adj — strict and serious in manner; plain and without decoration
中文释义：严峻的；朴素的；简朴的。

Examples:

  • The teacher had an austere manner.
  • His room was austere, with only a bed and a desk.

Common Phrases:

  • austere lifestyle — 简朴的生活方式
  • austere expression — 严肃表情

Synonyms:

  • severe
  • strict
  • plain

5. capricious /kəˈprɪʃəs/

adj — changing suddenly for no reason; unpredictable; impulsive
中文释义：反复无常的；任性的；多变的。

Examples:

  • The weather here is extremely capricious.
  • She is known for her capricious decisions.

Common Phrases:

  • capricious behavior — 任性行为
  • capricious changes — 反复无常的变化

Synonyms:

  • unpredictable
  • fickle
  • impulsive

6. defamatory /dɪˈfæmətɔːri/

adj — damaging someone’s reputation by false statements
中文释义：诽谤的；中伤的。

Examples:

  • The article contained defamatory remarks.
  • He sued the newspaper for publishing a defamatory story.

Common Phrases:

  • defamatory statement — 诽谤性的言论
  • defamatory content — 诽谤内容

Synonyms:

  • slanderous
  • libelous
  • malicious

7. esoteric /ˌesəˈterɪk/

adj — understood by only a small number of people with special knowledge
中文释义：深奥的；难懂的；只有内行才懂的。

Examples:

  • The philosopher’s ideas are too esoteric for most readers.
  • He gave an esoteric explanation that puzzled the audience.

Common Phrases:

  • esoteric knowledge — 深奥知识
  • esoteric concepts — 深奥概念

Synonyms:

  • obscure
  • mysterious
  • arcane

Word Root:

  • 来自希腊语 esōterikos → “内部的、秘密的” → 深奥、隐晦
