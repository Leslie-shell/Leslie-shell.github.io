Advanced Vocabulary in 60 Minutes-1

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Vocabulary 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.8k 阅读时长 ≈ 3 分钟
ubiquitous |

参考:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLN6IdRtDhg

1. ubiquitous /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/

adj — present, appearing, or found everywhere
中文释义：无处不在的。

Examples:

  • Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern society.
  • His influence was ubiquitous throughout the industry.

Common Phrases:

  • ubiquitous presence — 无处不在的存在
  • ubiquitous technology — 普及的技术

Synonyms:

  • pervasive
  • widespread
  • omnipresent

Word Root:

  • ubi-（拉丁语：where）
  • ubiquity — 普遍存在

2. cumbersome /ˈkʌmbərsəm/

adj — large or heavy and therefore difficult to carry or use
中文释义：繁重的；笨重的。

Examples:

  • The equipment is too cumbersome to move.
  • The process was slow and cumbersome.

Common Phrases:

  • cumbersome procedure — 繁琐的流程
  • cumbersome equipment — 笨重设备

Synonyms:

  • unwieldy
  • clumsy
  • bulky

3. facetious /fəˈsiːʃəs/

adj — treating serious issues with deliberately inappropriate humor
中文释义：滑稽的；乱开玩笑的。

Examples:

  • He made a facetious remark at the wrong time.
  • Stop being facetious; this is serious.

Common Phrases:

  • facetious comment — 滑稽的评论
  • facetious tone — 玩笑语气

Synonyms:

  • humorous
  • playful
  • joking

4. arduous /ˈɑːrdʒuəs/

adj — very difficult; requiring a lot of effort
中文释义：艰巨的；费力的。

Examples:

  • Climbing the mountain was an arduous task.
  • It takes arduous work to achieve success.

Common Phrases:

  • arduous journey — 艰辛的旅程
  • arduous process — 艰难过程

Synonyms:

  • difficult
  • exhausting
  • demanding

5. presumptuous /prɪˈzʌmptʃuəs/

adj — showing too much confidence; disrespectfully bold
中文释义：放肆的；冒昧的。

Examples:

  • It was presumptuous of him to assume he’d be invited.
  • Don’t be presumptuous when dealing with clients.

Common Phrases:

  • presumptuous attitude — 放肆态度
  • presumptuous behavior — 冒昧行为

Synonyms:

  • arrogant
  • bold
  • overconfident

6. pensive /ˈpensɪv/

adj — deeply or seriously thoughtful
中文释义：沉思的；忧郁的。

Examples:

  • She sat in a pensive mood by the window.
  • His pensive expression suggested he was lost in thought.

Common Phrases:

  • pensive look — 沉思的样子
  • pensive silence — 沉默的沉思

Synonyms:

  • thoughtful
  • reflective
  • contemplative

7. circuitous /sərˈkjuːɪtəs/

adj — longer than the most direct way; roundabout
中文释义：迂回的；绕行的。

Examples:

  • He took a circuitous route home.
  • Her explanation was circuitous and confusing.

Common Phrases:

  • circuitous path — 迂回的道路
  • circuitous explanation — 兜圈子的解释

Synonyms:

  • indirect
  • roundabout
  • winding

8. conscientious /ˌkɑːnʃiˈenʃəs/

adj — very careful and responsible; done with great attention
中文释义：认真负责的；尽责的。

Examples:

  • She is a conscientious worker.
  • He makes conscientious efforts to improve.

Common Phrases:

  • conscientious student — 认真学习的学生
  • conscientious effort — 尽责努力

Synonyms:

  • responsible
  • diligent
  • careful

9. meticulous /məˈtɪkjələs/

adj — extremely careful with details
中文释义：细致的；一丝不苟的。

Examples:

  • He is meticulous about his work.
  • The plan was prepared with meticulous attention to detail.

Common Phrases:

  • meticulous planning — 周密计划
  • meticulous details — 极其细致的细节

Synonyms:

  • detailed
  • precise
  • thorough

10. convoluted /ˈkɑːnvəluːtɪd/

adj — extremely complex and difficult to follow
中文释义：复杂的；盘绕的；晦涩的。

Examples:

  • The story has a convoluted plot.
  • His explanation was so convoluted that no one understood.

Common Phrases:

  • convoluted reasoning — 复杂的推理
  • convoluted design — 复杂的设计

Synonyms:

  • complex
  • complicated
  • tangled
