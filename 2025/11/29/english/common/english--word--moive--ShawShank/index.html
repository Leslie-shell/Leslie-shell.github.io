《肖申克的救赎》核心词汇整理-

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Moive 阅读次数：
本文字数： 1.7k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟

Core Themes

  • Redemption /rɪˈdempʃn/
    • Meaning: The act of being saved from sin, error, or evil; in the movie, it represents Andy’s moral and emotional liberation.
    • Example: The film’s title suggests that Shawshank is a place where redemption is possible, even in darkness.
  • Hope vs. Despair
    • Hope: Andy’s famous line: “Hope is a good thing, maybe the best of things.“
    • Despair: Brooks’ suicide shows the danger of losing hope.
  • Institutionalization /ˌɪnstɪˌtuːʃənəlaɪˈzeɪʃn/
    • Meaning: The psychological effect of long-term imprisonment, making freedom terrifying.
    • Example: Red says, “These walls are funny. First you hate ‘em, then you get used to ‘em.”

2. Prison Life Vocabulary

  • Parole /pəˈroʊl/: Conditional release from prison.
    • Example: Red is denied parole multiple times because he doesn’t “reform.”
  • Warden /ˈwɔːrdn/: The prison’s chief administrator.
    • Example: The warden pretends to care about justice but is corrupt.
  • Solitary Confinement /ˈsɑːlətɛri kənˈfaɪnmənt/: Punishment in isolation.
    • Example: Andy spends a month in solitary for playing Mozart over the speakers.

3. Key Plot Terms

  • Escape /ɪˈskeɪp/: Andy’s meticulous plan to break free.
    • Phrase: “A escape that took 19 years to plan.”
  • Innocence /ˈɪnəsns/: Andy’s wrongful conviction.
    • Example: “I didn’t kill my wife… but I wish I had.”
  • Perseverance /ˌpɜːrsəˈvɪrəns/: Andy’s slow progress with the rock hammer.
    • Quote: “Geology is the study of pressure and time.”

4. Famous Quotes & Phrases

  1. “Get busy living, or get busy dying.”
    • Grammar: Imperative mood (command).
  2. “Some birds aren’t meant to be caged.”
    • Metaphor: Freedom is innate.
  3. “Fear can hold you prisoner. Hope can set you free.”

5. Words for Character Traits

  • Resourceful (Andy): Clever at solving problems (e.g., creating a fake identity).
  • Resilient (Red): Able to recover after decades in prison.
  • Corrupt (Warden Norton): Willing to break laws for power.
