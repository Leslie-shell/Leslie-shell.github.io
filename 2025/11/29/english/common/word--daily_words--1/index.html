1. classic /ˈklæsɪk/

adj — judged over a period of time to be of the highest quality and outstanding of its kind
中文释义：经典的；典型的。

Examples:

  • She wore a classic little black dress to the party.
  • That movie is considered a classic in cinema history.

Common Phrases:

  • classic example — 典型例子
  • classic design — 经典设计
  • classic literature — 经典文学

Synonyms:

  • timeless
  • traditional
  • exemplary

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 classicus → first-class → 典型、经典

2. explore /ɪkˈsplɔːr/

v — to examine or investigate something carefully
中文释义：探索；探究。

Examples:

  • They plan to explore the ancient ruins next summer.
  • Scientists continue to explore the effects of climate change.

Common Phrases:

  • explore possibilities — 探索可能性
  • explore ideas — 探讨想法
  • space exploration — 太空探索

Synonyms:

  • investigate
  • examine
  • research

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 explorare → 检查、侦查 → 探索

3. portrait /ˈpɔːrtrət/

n — a painting, drawing, or photograph of a person; description of a character
中文释义：肖像；人物描写。

Examples:

  • The artist painted a beautiful portrait of his wife.
  • The novel provides a vivid portrait of rural life.

Common Phrases:

  • family portrait — 家庭肖像
  • character portrait — 人物描写
  • self-portrait — 自画像

Synonyms:

  • image
  • depiction
  • representation

Word Root:

  • 来自法语 portrait → 描绘 → 肖像

4. landscape /ˈlændskeɪp/

n — all the visible features of an area of land; natural scenery
中文释义：风景；地貌。

Examples:

  • The landscape of the countryside was breathtaking.
  • Photographers often capture landscapes at sunrise.

Common Phrases:

  • rural landscape — 乡村风景
  • urban landscape — 城市景观
  • landscape painting — 风景画

Synonyms:

  • scenery
  • view
  • panorama

Word Root:

  • 来自荷兰语 landschap → 描绘土地 → 风景

5. brand /brænd/

n — a type of product made by a particular company; a kind or category
中文释义：品牌；类型。

Examples:

  • This brand of coffee is my favorite.
  • They launched a new brand of sportswear.

Common Phrases:

  • well-known brand — 知名品牌
  • luxury brand — 奢侈品牌
  • brand image — 品牌形象

Synonyms:

  • label
  • trademark
  • make

Word Root:

  • 来自古英语 brand → 烙印 → 品牌

6. stunningly /ˈstʌnɪŋli/

adv — in a way that is extremely impressive or surprising
中文释义：极其；惊人地。

Examples:

  • She was stunningly beautiful in that red dress.
  • The performance was stunningly executed.

Common Phrases:

  • stunningly beautiful — 极其美丽
  • stunningly effective — 非常有效
  • stunningly obvious — 极其明显

Synonyms:

  • remarkably
  • extraordinarily
  • impressively

Word Root:

  • 来自 stun → 使震惊 → 极其惊人地

7. stunning /ˈstʌnɪŋ/

adj — extremely impressive, attractive, or surprising
中文释义：极好的；惊人的。

Examples:

  • The view from the mountaintop was absolutely stunning.
  • She received a stunning compliment.

Common Phrases:

  • stunning view — 惊人的景色
  • stunning result — 惊人的结果
  • stunning appearance — 惊人的外表

Synonyms:

  • amazing
  • gorgeous
  • breathtaking

Word Root:

  • 来自 stun → 使震惊 → 极好的

8. noticeable /ˈnoʊtɪsəbl/

adj — easy to see or recognize
中文释义：明显的；显著的。

Examples:

  • There was a noticeable improvement in her English.
  • A noticeable change in temperature occurred overnight.

Common Phrases:

  • highly noticeable — 非常明显
  • noticeable difference — 明显差异
  • noticeable effect — 显著影响

Synonyms:

  • evident
  • observable
  • perceptible

Word Root:

  • 来自 notice → 注意 → 明显的

9. material /məˈtɪriəl/

n — the matter from which a thing is made; substance
中文释义：材料；原料。

Examples:

  • This dress is made of high-quality material.
  • The factory uses recycled material to produce paper.

Common Phrases:

  • raw material — 原材料
  • construction material — 建筑材料
  • study material — 学习资料

Synonyms:

  • substance
  • fabric
  • component

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 materialis → 物质 → 材料
