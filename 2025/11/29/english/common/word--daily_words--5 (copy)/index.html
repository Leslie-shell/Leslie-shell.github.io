1. ubiquitous /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/

adj — present, appearing, or found everywhere
中文释义: 无处不在的

Examples:

  • Smartphones are ubiquitous in modern life.
  • The brand became ubiquitous after the ad campaign.

Common Phrases:

  • ubiquitous presence — 无处不在
  • ubiquitous technology — 普及技术
  • increasingly ubiquitous — 日益普及

Synonyms:

  • omnipresent
  • pervasive
  • universal

2. essential /ɪˈsenʃ(ə)l/

adj — absolutely necessary; extremely important
中文释义: 基本的；必要的

Examples:

  • Water is essential for survival.
  • Reading is essential for learning a language.

Common Phrases:

  • essential skills — 必要技能
  • essential requirements — 基本要求
  • essential component — 必要组成部分

Synonyms:

  • necessary
  • crucial
  • vital

3. upbeat /ˈʌpbiːt/

adj — cheerful and optimistic
中文释义: 乐观的

  • She kept an upbeat attitude despite setbacks.
  • The mood was upbeat throughout the celebration.

Common Phrases:

  • upbeat mood — 乐观心情
  • upbeat attitude — 积极态度
  • stay upbeat — 保持乐观

Synonyms:

  • cheerful
  • positive
  • optimistic

4. resume /rɪˈzuːm/ (v.) | /ˈrezəmeɪ/ (n.)

v — begin again after a pause; n — a brief account of one’s education, work experience, and qualifications
中文释义: (v.) 恢复；(n.) 简历

Examples:

  • He will resume work soon.
  • Her resume impressed the HR.

Common Phrases:

  • resume work — 恢复工作
  • job resume — 求职简历
  • resume activities — 重新开始活动

Synonyms:

  • (v.) continue, restart
  • (n.) CV, curriculum vitae

Synonyms:

  • requirement
  • standard
  • criterion
