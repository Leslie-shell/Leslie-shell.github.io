1. latitude /ˈlætɪtjuːd/

n — the distance north or south of the equator, measured in degrees
中文释义：纬度。

Examples:

  • The tropical rainforests are located near the equator at low latitudes.
  • High latitudes experience colder climates.

Common Phrases:

  • low latitude — 低纬度
  • high latitude — 高纬度
  • latitude line — 纬线

Synonyms:

  • parallel
  • geographic coordinate

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 latitudo → 宽度 → 纬度

2. horizon /həˈraɪzən/

n — the line at which the earth’s surface and the sky appear to meet
中文释义：地平线。

Examples:

  • The sun disappeared below the horizon in a spectacular sunset.
  • Sailors watched the horizon carefully for approaching ships.

Common Phrases:

  • beyond the horizon — 在地平线之外
  • horizon line — 地平线

Synonyms:

  • skyline
  • view

Word Root:

  • 来自希腊语 horizōn → 限制、边界 → 地平线

3. altitude /ˈæltɪtjuːd/

n — the height of an object or point in relation to sea level or ground level
中文释义：海拔高度。

Examples:

  • The airplane reached a cruising altitude of 35,000 feet.
  • Mount Everest has the highest altitude in the world.

Common Phrases:

  • high altitude — 高海拔
  • altitude sickness — 高原反应

Synonyms:

  • height
  • elevation

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 altitudo → 高度 → 海拔

4. disaster /dɪˈzæstər/

n — a sudden event that causes great damage or loss of life
中文释义：灾难。

Examples:

  • The earthquake was one of the worst natural disasters in history.
  • Floods and hurricanes are common natural disasters.

Common Phrases:

  • natural disaster — 自然灾害
  • disaster relief — 灾难救援

Synonyms:

  • catastrophe
  • calamity
  • tragedy

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 disaster → 不幸的星象 → 灾难

5. mishap /ˈmɪshæp/

n — a minor accident or unlucky event
中文释义：小事故。

Examples:

  • Our camping trip went smoothly except for a minor mishap with the tent.
  • She laughed off the mishap with her coffee spill.

Common Phrases:

  • minor mishap — 小事故
  • unfortunate mishap — 不幸的小意外

Synonyms:

  • accident
  • misfortune

Word Root:

  • mis-（错误） + hap（运气） → 运气不佳 → 小事故

6. catastrophic /ˌkætəˈstrɒfɪk/

adj — causing sudden and severe damage or suffering
中文释义：灾难性的。

Examples:

  • Climate change could have catastrophic consequences for coastal cities.
  • The hurricane was catastrophic for the small town.

Common Phrases:

  • catastrophic event — 灾难性事件
  • catastrophic failure — 灾难性失败

Synonyms:

  • disastrous
  • ruinous
  • devastating

Word Root:

  • 来自希腊语 katastrophē → 翻转 → 灾难

7. endanger /ɪnˈdeɪndʒər/

v — to put someone or something at risk or in danger
中文释义：危及；使遭受危险。

Examples:

  • Poaching continues to endanger many wildlife species.
  • Driving recklessly can endanger lives.

Common Phrases:

  • endanger wildlife — 危及野生动物
  • endanger safety — 危及安全

Synonyms:

  • jeopardize
  • threaten
  • imperil

Word Root:

  • 来自古法语 en + danger → 置于危险 → 危及

8. jeopardise /ˈdʒepədaɪz/

v — to put someone or something in a situation where they could be harmed or fail
中文释义：危害；危及（英式拼写）。

Examples:

  • Smoking can seriously jeopardise your health.
  • Ignoring safety rules may jeopardise the project.

Common Phrases:

  • jeopardise health — 危害健康
  • jeopardise success — 危及成功

Synonyms:

  • endanger
  • threaten
  • risk

Word Root:

  • 来自古法语 jeu parti → 未定的局 → 危害

9. destructive /dɪˈstrʌktɪv/

adj — causing great harm or damage
中文释义：破坏性的。

Examples:

  • The hurricane caused destructive winds and flooding.
  • Vandalism is destructive to public property.

Common Phrases:

  • destructive force — 破坏力
  • destructive behavior — 破坏性行为

Synonyms:

  • damaging
  • harmful
  • ruinous

Word Root:

  • 来自拉丁语 destruere → 拆毁 → 破坏

10. greenhouse /ˈɡriːnhaʊs/

n — a building made of glass where plants are grown; or gases that trap heat in the atmosphere
中文释义：温室；温室效应。

Examples:

  • Greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide contribute to global warming.
  • Tomatoes grow well in a greenhouse.

Common Phrases:

  • greenhouse gas — 温室气体
  • greenhouse effect — 温室效应

Synonyms:

  • glasshouse
  • hotbed (for metaphorical use)

Word Root:

  • 来自英语 green + house → 种植物的温室
