In this life, what did you miss?
The wife asked the husband when she was 25. Despondently, the husband replied: ‘I missed a new job opportunity.’
When she was 35, the husband angrily told her that he had just missed the bus.
At 45, the husband sadly said: ‘I missed the oppotunity seeing my closed relative before his last breath.’
At 55, the husband said disappointingly: ‘I missed a good chance to retire.’
At 65, the husband hurriedly replied: ‘I missed a dental appointment.’
At 75, the wife did not ask the husband anymore, the husband was kneeling in front of the very sick wife. Remembering the question the wife used to ask him, this time he asked the wife the same question. The wife, with a smile and peaceful look, replied: ‘In this life, I did not miss having you!’
The husband was full of tears. He always thought that they could be together forever. He was always busy with work and trifles. So much so he had never been thoughtful to his wife. The husband hugged the wife tightly and said: ‘Over 50 years, how I had allowed myself to miss your deep love for me.’
In the busy city life, there are many people who are always busy with work. These people revolve their lives around their jobs, these people sacrifice(牺牲) all their times and health to meet the social expectations. They are unwilling to spend times on health care. They miss the opportunity to be with their children in their growing up. They neglect (忽视)the loved ones who care for them, and also their health.

Nobody knows what is going to happen one year from now.
Life is not permanent, so always live in the now. Express your gratitude to your loved ones in words. Show your care with actions. Treat everyday as the last episode of life. In this way, when you are gone, you loved ones would have nothing to feel sorry about.

Words

1. despondently /dɪˈspɒndəntli/

adv — in a way that shows you are very unhappy, without hope
中文释义: 沮丧地；灰心地。

Examples:

  • He replied despondently, realizing he had missed a new job opportunity.
  • She walked away despondently after the disappointing news.

Synonyms:

  • hopelessly
  • gloomily

Word Root:

  • Root: despondent（沮丧的）+ -ly 副词后缀

2. opportunity /ˌɒpərˈtjuːnəti/

n — a time when a particular situation makes it possible to do or achieve something
中文释义: 机会；时机。

Examples:

  • I missed the opportunity to see my close relative.
  • This job offers opportunities for career growth.

Common Phrases:

  • job opportunity — 工作机会
  • seize an opportunity — 抓住机会

Synonyms:

  • chance
  • possibility

3. disappointingly /ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪntɪŋli/

adv — in a way that makes you feel disappointed
中文释义: 令人失望地。

Examples:

  • “I missed a good chance to retire,” he said disappointingly.
  • The results were disappointingly low.

Related Words:

  • disappointment (n.) 失望
  • disappointing (adj.) 令人失望的

4. kneeling /ˈniːlɪŋ/

v — present participle of kneel: to be in or move into a position where your knees are on the ground
中文释义: 跪着；跪下。

Examples:

  • The husband was kneeling beside his very sick wife.
  • People were kneeling in prayer.

Related Forms:

  • kneel (v.) 下跪
  • knelt (past)

5. trifles /ˈtraɪflz/

n — unimportant or trivial things
中文释义: 琐事；小事。

Examples:

  • He was always busy with work and trifles.
  • Don’t worry about such trifles.

Synonyms:

  • trivialities
  • small matters

6. revolve around /rɪˈvɒlv əˌraʊnd/

phrasal v — to have somebody/something as the main interest or subject
中文释义: 围绕…旋转；以…为中心。

Examples:

  • Their lives revolve around their jobs.
  • The discussion revolved around economic issues.

Synonyms:

  • center on
  • focus on

7. sacrifice /ˈsækrɪfaɪs/

v — to give up something important or valuable to get or achieve something else
中文释义: 牺牲；放弃。

Examples:

  • They sacrifice their health to meet social expectations.
  • Parents often sacrifice their own needs for their children.

Related Forms:

  • sacrifice (n.) 牺牲
  • sacrificial (adj.) 牺牲的

8. neglect /nɪˈɡlɛkt/

v — to not give enough attention to something or someone
中文释义: 忽视；疏忽。

Examples:

  • They neglect the loved ones who care for them.
  • The garden was neglected for years.

Synonyms:

  • ignore
  • overlook

9. permanent /ˈpɜːrmənənt/

adj — lasting for a long time or for all time; existing all the time
中文释义: 永久的；永恒的。

Examples:

  • Life is not permanent, so live in the present.
  • She decided to make the change permanent.

Synonyms:

  • lasting
  • enduring

10. gratitude /ˈɡrætɪtjuːd/

n — the feeling of being grateful and wanting to express thanks
中文释义: 感激；感谢。

Examples:

  • Express your gratitude to loved ones in words.
  • He showed deep gratitude for their help.

Common Phrases:

  • express gratitude — 表达感谢
  • deep gratitude — 深深的感激

Synonyms:

  • appreciation
  • thankfulness

Pharse

1. miss the opportunity — 错过机会

Example:
He missed the opportunity to see his relative.
他错过了见亲人的机会。

2. revolve around — 围绕…旋转；以…为中心

Example:
Their lives revolve around work.
他们的生活以工作为中心。

3. sacrifice health — 牺牲健康

Example:
They sacrifice their health to meet expectations.
他们为了满足期望而牺牲了健康。

4. neglect loved ones — 忽视爱人 / 亲人

Example:
Don’t neglect loved ones while busy with work.
即使工作再忙，也不要忽视亲人。

5. live in the now — 活在当下

Example:
Life is not permanent, so always live in the now.
人生无常，所以要活在当下。

6. express gratitude — 表达感谢

Example:
Express your gratitude to your loved ones in words.
把你的感谢用语言表达给你爱的人。

7. treat every day as the last — 把每一天当作最后一天

Example:
Treat every day as the last episode of life.
把每一天当作生命的最后一集去过。

8. nothing to feel sorry about — 毫无遗憾

Example:
Then your loved ones will have nothing to feel sorry about.
这样你所爱的人就不会有任何遗憾。

总结

  1. 人生无常：一年后甚至一小时后发生的事都不可预测 → 珍惜当下。
  2. 爱与陪伴比物质更重要：不要因忙碌工作错失家人和伴侣的爱。
  3. 表达与行动并行：感谢爱人、关心亲人，不要只是心里想着。
  4. 生活要有觉察：把每一天当作最后的篇章 → 活得专注而充实。
  5. 平衡工作与生活：不要让琐事、社会压力完全占据人生。
