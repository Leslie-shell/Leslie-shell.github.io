1. dimension /dɪˈmenʃən/

n — a measurable extent of some kind, such as length, breadth, depth, or height
中文释义: 方面；尺寸

Examples:

  • We need to consider every dimension of the problem.
  • The room has impressive dimensions.

Common Phrases:

  • social dimension — 社会方面
  • economic dimension — 经济方面

Synonyms:

  • aspect
  • measure
  • extent

Word Root:

  • Root: dimens- (Latin: “to measure”)
  • Suffix: -ion 表示名词
  • Formation: dimens + -ion → dimension

2. maintenance /ˈmeɪntənəns/

n — the process of maintaining or preserving someone or something
中文释义: 维护；保养

Examples:

  • Regular maintenance of machinery is essential.
  • The house requires constant maintenance.

Common Phrases:

  • routine maintenance — 日常维护
  • maintenance work — 维护工作

Synonyms:

  • upkeep
  • servicing
  • preservation

Word Root:

  • Root: ten- (Latin: “to hold”)
  • Prefix: main- → 保持
  • Formation: main + tenance → maintenance

3. loose /luːs/

adj — not firmly or tightly fixed in place
中文释义: 松动的；不牢固的

Examples:

  • The screw is loose and needs tightening.
  • She wore a loose dress for comfort.

Common Phrases:

  • loose connection — 松动的连接
  • loose fitting — 宽松的衣服

Synonyms:

  • slack
  • unfastened
  • free

Word Root:

  • Origin: Old English los “not tight”

4. tableau /ˈtæbloʊ/

n — a graphic description or representation; a striking scene
中文释义: 绘画；画面

Examples:

  • The artist painted a tableau of village life.
  • The play ended with a dramatic tableau.

Common Phrases:

  • dramatic tableau — 戏剧性的画面
  • visual tableau — 视觉画面

Synonyms:

  • picture
  • scene
  • representation

Word Root:

  • Origin: French tableau “picture, painting”

5. portfolio /pɔːrtˈfoʊlioʊ/

n — a collection of works or documents
中文释义: 文件夹；作品集

Examples:

  • He presented his portfolio to the potential clients.
  • The artist’s portfolio contains his best paintings.

Common Phrases:

  • investment portfolio — 投资组合
  • creative portfolio — 创意作品集

Synonyms:

  • collection
  • binder
  • dossier

Word Root:

  • Root: port- (Latin: “to carry”)
  • Suffix: -folio “leaf, sheet”

6. assurance /əˈʃʊərəns/

n — a positive declaration intended to give confidence; a guarantee
中文释义: 保证；担保

Examples:

  • The company gives assurance of quality.
  • He gave me his assurance that the work would be finished on time.

Common Phrases:

  • life assurance — 人寿保险
  • assurance of success — 成功的保证

Synonyms:

  • guarantee
  • promise
  • pledge

Word Root:

  • Root: sure (Latin: “certain”)
  • Suffix: -ance 表示名词

7. integrate /ˈɪntɪɡreɪt/

v — combine (one thing) with another so that they become a whole
中文释义: 整合；合并

Examples:

  • The new system integrates all company functions.
  • They tried to integrate technology into the classroom.

Common Phrases:

  • integrate into — 整合进
  • fully integrated — 完全整合的

Synonyms:

  • unify
  • combine
  • incorporate

Word Root:

  • Root: integer (Latin: “whole”)

8. sandbox /ˈsændbɑːks/

n — a contained area for playing or testing
中文释义: 沙箱；测试区

Examples:

  • Children were playing in the sandbox.
  • Developers used a sandbox to test the software safely.

Common Phrases:

  • testing sandbox — 测试沙箱
  • sandbox environment — 沙箱环境

Synonyms:

  • play area
  • test area

9. specification /ˌspesɪfɪˈkeɪʃən/

n — an exact statement of particulars
中文释义: 规格；说明书

Examples:

  • Follow the technical specifications.
  • The machine was built according to the specifications.

Common Phrases:

  • product specification — 产品规格
  • technical specification — 技术规格

Synonyms:

  • detail
  • requirement
  • description

Word Root:

  • Root: spec- (Latin: “to look”)
  • Suffix: -ation 表示名词

10. precise /prɪˈsaɪs/

adj — marked by exactness and accuracy of expression or detail
中文释义: 精确的；准确的

Examples:

  • Measure the ingredients with precise accuracy.
  • The instructions must be precise.

Common Phrases:

  • precise measurement — 精确测量
  • precise time — 精确时间

Synonyms:

  • exact
  • accurate
  • correct

Word Root:

  • Root: pre- (Latin: “before”) + cis- (cut) → “cut exactly”
