1. material /məˈtɪriəl/

n — the matter from which a thing is or can be made
中文释义: 材料；原料

Examples:

  • The dress is made of high-quality material.
  • Wood is a common construction material.

Common Phrases:

  • raw material — 原材料
  • construction material — 建筑材料

Synonyms:

  • substance
  • fabric
  • component

Word Root:

  • Root: mater- (Latin: “matter, substance”)

2. suffix /ˈsʌfɪks/ & prefix /ˈpriːfɪks/

n — a letter or group of letters added at the end (suffix) or beginning (prefix) of a word
中文释义: 后缀和前缀

Examples:

  • The suffix “-ness” forms nouns from adjectives.
  • “Un-“ is a common prefix meaning “not”.

Common Phrases:

  • word prefix — 单词前缀
  • grammatical suffix — 语法后缀

Synonyms:

  • affix
  • ending / beginning

Word Root:

  • Prefix: pre- “before”
  • Suffix: -fix “attach”

3. intermediate /ˌɪntərˈmiːdiət/

adj — in the middle; between two points, levels, or stages
中文释义: 中间的；中级的

Examples:

  • He is an intermediate student in English.
  • The bridge serves as an intermediate stop between cities.

Common Phrases:

  • intermediate level — 中级水平
  • intermediate stage — 中间阶段

Synonyms:

  • middle
  • mid-level
  • median

Word Root:

  • Root: inter- (Latin: “between”)

4. sentimental /ˌsentɪˈmentl/

adj — relating to feelings, especially showing tenderness or nostalgia
中文释义: 多愁善感的

Examples:

  • She became sentimental while looking at old photos.
  • The movie was too sentimental for my taste.

Common Phrases:

  • sentimental value — 情感价值
  • sentimental journey — 情感之旅

Synonyms:

  • emotional
  • nostalgic
  • tender

Word Root:

  • Root: sent- (Latin: “feel”)

5. refrain /rɪˈfreɪn/

v — stop oneself from doing something
中文释义: 避免；克制

Examples:

  • Please refrain from smoking in this area.
  • She refrained from commenting on the matter.

Common Phrases:

  • refrain from — 避免做某事

Synonyms:

  • abstain
  • avoid
  • withhold

Word Root:

  • Root: frain (Old French: “restrain”)

6. essential /ɪˈsenʃl/

adj — absolutely necessary; extremely important
中文释义: 基本的；必要的

Examples:

  • Water is essential for life.
  • Good communication skills are essential in any job.

Common Phrases:

  • essential skills — 必备技能
  • essential requirement — 必要条件

Synonyms:

  • necessary
  • fundamental
  • crucial

Word Root:

  • Root: essent- (Latin: “to be”)

7. employable /ɪmˈplɔɪəbl/

adj — having the necessary skills to be employed
中文释义: 可就业的

Examples:

  • Graduates need to be employable in the modern job market.
  • The course improves students’ employable skills.

Common Phrases:

  • employable skills — 就业能力
  • employable graduate — 就业毕业生

Synonyms:

  • job-ready
  • marketable
  • qualified

Word Root:

  • Root: ploy (Latin: “to fold, use”)
  • Prefix: em- “put into”

8. scale /skeɪl/

n — the relative size or extent of something; a system of measurement
中文释义: 规模；尺度

Examples:

  • The project is on a large scale.
  • Use a scale to measure the ingredients accurately.

Common Phrases:

  • on a large scale — 大规模
  • scale of measurement — 测量尺度

Synonyms:

  • size
  • magnitude
  • level

Word Root:

  • Root: scala (Latin: “ladder, steps”)

9. auxiliary /ɔːɡˈzɪljəri/

adj — providing supplementary or additional help
中文释义: 辅助的

Examples:

  • Auxiliary staff supported the main team.
  • The auxiliary engine provides backup power.

Common Phrases:

  • auxiliary equipment — 辅助设备
  • auxiliary staff — 辅助人员

Synonyms:

  • additional
  • supplementary
  • supportive

Word Root:

  • Root: auxilium (Latin: “help, aid”)

10. derive /dɪˈraɪv/

v — obtain something from (a source or origin)
中文释义: 派生；来源

Examples:

  • Many English words derive from Latin.
  • He derives great satisfaction from teaching.

Common Phrases:

  • derive from — 来源于
  • derive benefit — 获得好处

Synonyms:

  • obtain
  • originate
  • stem

Word Root:

  • Root: rivus (Latin: “stream”)

11. exposure /ɪkˈspoʊʒər/

n — the state of being exposed to contact with something
中文释义: 接触；暴露

Examples:

  • Prolonged exposure to sunlight can damage your skin.
  • The exhibition provided exposure to modern art.

Common Phrases:

  • exposure to risk — 风险暴露
  • media exposure — 媒体曝光

Synonyms:

  • contact
  • experience
  • vulnerability

Word Root:

  • Root: ex- “out” + ponere “to place”
