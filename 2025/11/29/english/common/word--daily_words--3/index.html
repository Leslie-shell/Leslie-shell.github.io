1. undertaking /ˈʌndərteɪkɪŋ/

n — a task, project, or responsibility that someone commits to; a formal pledge
中文释义：承诺；事业，任务

Examples:

  • Building a bridge across the river is a major undertaking.
  • He undertook the difficult task with determination.

Common Phrases:

  • a difficult undertaking — 艰巨的任务
  • give an undertaking — 作出承诺

Synonyms:

  • task
  • project
  • commitment

Word Root:

  • Root: undertake → 承担
  • Suffix: -ing → 名词化
  • Formation: undertake + -ing → undertaking

2. mission /ˈmɪʃn/

n — an important task or purpose assigned to a person or group
中文释义：使命；任务

Examples:

  • Their mission is to provide clean water to the village.
  • He accepted the mission with great enthusiasm.

Common Phrases:

  • mission impossible — 不可能的任务
  • life mission — 人生使命

Synonyms:

  • assignment
  • duty
  • task

Word Root:

  • Root: miss- (Latin: “to send”) → 派遣、送
  • Formation: miss + -ion → mission

3. operation /ˌɑːpəˈreɪʃn/

n — the act of working, functioning, or performing a process; a surgical procedure
中文释义：手术；运作，操作

Examples:

  • The patient is recovering well after the operation.
  • Business operations have improved this quarter.

Common Phrases:

  • medical operation — 医疗手术
  • business operation — 业务运作

Synonyms:

  • procedure
  • process
  • activity

Word Root:

  • Root: oper- (Latin: “work”) → 工作、操作
  • Suffix: -ation → 名词
  • Formation: oper + -ation → operation

4. labor /ˈleɪbər/

n — physical or mental work; workforce
中文释义：劳动；劳动力

Examples:

  • Many jobs still require manual labor.
  • Labor costs have increased this year.

Common Phrases:

  • manual labor — 体力劳动
  • labor cost — 劳动力成本

Synonyms:

  • work
  • toil
  • employment

Word Root:

  • Root: labor- (Latin: “work”) → 工作
  • Formation: labor → labor

5. disseminate /dɪˈsemɪneɪt/

v — to spread information, knowledge, or ideas widely
中文释义：传播，散布

Examples:

  • The internet helps to disseminate information quickly.
  • Scientists disseminate research findings through journals.

Common Phrases:

  • disseminate knowledge — 传播知识
  • disseminate information — 散布信息

Synonyms:

  • spread
  • broadcast
  • circulate

Word Root:

  • Root: semin- (Latin: “seed”) → 播种
  • Prefix: dis- → 分散
  • Formation: dis + semin + -ate → disseminate

6. reinforce /ˌriːɪnˈfɔːrs/

v — to strengthen or support something
中文释义：加强，巩固

Examples:

  • The teacher used examples to reinforce the lesson.
  • Security measures were reinforced after the incident.

Common Phrases:

  • reinforce an idea — 加强观点
  • reinforce security — 加强安全

Synonyms:

  • strengthen
  • bolster
  • support

Word Root:

  • Root: force → 力量
  • Prefix: re- → 再次
  • Formation: re + force → reinforce

7. comprehensive /ˌkɑːmprɪˈhensɪv/

adj — including all or nearly all elements or aspects of something
中文释义：全面的，综合的

Examples:

  • The report provides a comprehensive overview of the problem.
  • We need a comprehensive plan for the project.

Common Phrases:

  • comprehensive study — 综合研究
  • comprehensive understanding — 全面理解

Synonyms:

  • complete
  • thorough
  • extensive

Word Root:

  • Root: prehens- (Latin: “to grasp”) → 抓取
  • Prefix: com- → 全部
  • Formation: com + prehens + -ive → comprehensive
