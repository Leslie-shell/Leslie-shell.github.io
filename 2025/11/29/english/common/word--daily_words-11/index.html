neutral | anticipate | exhaust | permutation | prior | consolidate | parallelly | presenter | scenario

1. neutral /ˈnjuːtrəl/

adj — not supporting or helping either side in a conflict; impartial
中文释义: 中性的

Examples:

  • Switzerland remained neutral during the war.
  • Use neutral colors in your design for a professional look.

Common Phrases:

  • neutral stance — 中立立场
  • neutral color — 中性色

Synonyms:

  • impartial
  • unbiased
  • objective

Word Root:

  • Root: neuter (Latin: “neither”)

2. anticipate /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪt/

v — regard as probable; expect or predict
中文释义: 预料

Examples:

  • We anticipate a busy week ahead.
  • She anticipated that he would be late.

Common Phrases:

  • anticipate problems — 预料问题
  • anticipate results — 预期结果

Synonyms:

  • expect
  • foresee
  • predict

Word Root:

  • Prefix: ante- “before”
  • Root: capere “to take”

3. exhaust /ɪɡˈzɔːst/

v — drain of energy or resources; n — gases expelled from an engine
中文释义: 排气；使精疲力尽

Examples:

  • The long hike exhausted him.
  • The car’s exhaust contains harmful gases.

Common Phrases:

  • exhaust system — 排气系统
  • exhaust resources — 消耗资源

Synonyms:

  • drain
  • tire
  • emission

Word Root:

  • Prefix: ex- “out”
  • Root: haurire (Latin: “to draw out”)

4. permutation /ˌpɜːrmjʊˈteɪʃn/

n — a way in which a set or number of things can be ordered or arranged
中文释义: 排列

Examples:

  • There are six possible permutations of three objects.
  • Genetic permutations result in diverse traits.

Common Phrases:

  • permutation formula — 排列公式
  • permutation of elements — 元素排列

Synonyms:

  • arrangement
  • combination
  • order

Word Root:

  • Prefix: per- “through”
  • Root: mutare “to change”

5. prior /ˈpraɪər/

adj — existing or coming before in time, order, or importance
中文释义: 先前的；优先的

Examples:

  • Prior knowledge helps in learning new topics.
  • We must consider prior commitments.

Common Phrases:

  • prior experience — 先前经验
  • prior approval — 事先批准

Synonyms:

  • previous
  • earlier
  • preceding

Word Root:

  • Root: prior (Latin: “former, previous”)

6. consolidate /kənˈsɒlɪdeɪt/

v — combine into a single more effective or coherent whole
中文释义: 综合；巩固

Examples:

  • The company consolidated its offices into one building.
  • The manager consolidated all the reports for the meeting.

Common Phrases:

  • consolidate power — 巩固权力
  • consolidate resources — 整合资源

Synonyms:

  • combine
  • unify
  • strengthen

Word Root:

  • Prefix: con- “together”
  • Root: solidare (Latin: “to make solid”)

7. parallelly /ˈpærəlelɪ/

adv — occurring at the same time or in a corresponding way
中文释义: 并行地

Examples:

  • Tasks were processed parallelly to save time.
  • Multiple projects run parallelly in the company.

Common Phrases:

  • work parallelly — 并行工作
  • process parallelly — 并行处理

Synonyms:

  • concurrently
  • simultaneously
  • side by side

Word Root:

  • Root: parallelos (Greek: “beside one another”)

8. presenter /prɪˈzentər/

n — a person who introduces or hosts a program or event
中文释义: 主持人

Examples:

  • The TV presenter introduced the news segment.
  • She works as a presenter for a radio show.

Common Phrases:

  • show presenter — 节目主持人
  • news presenter — 新闻主持人

Synonyms:

  • host
  • anchor
  • announcer

Word Root:

  • Root: praesentare (Latin: “to show, display”)

9. scenario /sɪˈnɑːriəʊ/

n — a written outline of a film, play, or planned sequence of events; a possible situation
中文释义: 设想；场景

Examples:

  • We planned several scenarios for the project.
  • The worst-case scenario was considered.

Common Phrases:

  • scenario planning — 情景规划
  • hypothetical scenario — 假设情景

Synonyms:

  • situation
  • outline
  • plan

Word Root:

  • Root: scena (Latin: “stage, scene”)
