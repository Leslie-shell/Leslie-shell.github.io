English Word in use （Describing people） --elementray

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， WordInUse 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.6k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟

Appearance (外貌)

  1. tall /tɔːl/ 高的
    • My brother is very tall—he’s 195 cm!
  2. short /ʃɔːrt/ 矮的
    • She’s short but runs faster than me.
  3. thin /θɪn/ 瘦的
    • He’s thin because he exercises every day.
  4. fat /fæt/ 胖的 (Note: Use “overweight” or “curvy” for politeness.)
    • The doctor said I shouldn’t eat too much junk food or I’ll get fat.
  5. slim /slɪm/ 苗条的
    • She has a slim figure and wears stylish clothes.
  6. muscular /ˈmʌskjələr/ 肌肉发达的
    • The swimmer has strong, muscular arms.
  7. young /jʌŋ/ 年轻的
    • The young girl is learning to ride a bike.
  8. old /oʊld/ 年老的
    • The old man walks with a cane.
  9. handsome /ˈhænsəm/ 英俊的 (usually for men)
    • Her boyfriend is tall and handsome.
  10. beautiful /ˈbjuːtɪfl/ 美丽的
    • The sunset looks so beautiful tonight.
  11. pretty /ˈprɪti/ 漂亮的 (often for women/children)
    • Your daughter has pretty curly hair.
  12. ugly /ˈʌɡli/ 丑陋的 (impolite; use “unattractive” politely)
    • He thinks his old shoes are ugly.
  13. bald /bɔːld/ 秃头的
    • My uncle is bald but he jokes about it.
  14. long hair /lɒŋ her/ 长发
    • She braids her long hair every morning.
  15. short hair /ʃɔːrt her/ 短发
    • He prefers short hair because it’s easy to wash.

2. Face & Features (面部特征)

  1. round face /raʊnd feɪs/ 圆脸
    • Babies usually have cute round faces.
  2. square face /skwer feɪs/ 方脸
    • His square face makes him look serious.
  3. big eyes /bɪɡ aɪz/ 大眼睛
    • The cartoon character has huge big eyes.
  4. small eyes /smɔːl aɪz/ 小眼睛
    • She squints her small eyes in the sun.
  5. glasses /ˈɡlæsɪz/ 眼镜
    • She can’t read without her glasses.

3. Personality (性格)

  1. friendly /ˈfrendli/ 友好的
    • The new neighbor is very friendly.
  2. shy /ʃaɪ/ 害羞的
    • The shy boy hid behind his mother.
  3. confident /ˈkɒnfɪdənt/ 自信的
    • She gave a confident speech.
  4. kind /kaɪnd/ 善良的
    • He’s kind to animals and children.
  5. mean /miːn/ 刻薄的
    • The mean boss yelled at his employees.
  6. funny /ˈfʌni/ 有趣的
    • Everyone laughs at his funny jokes.
  7. serious /ˈsɪəriəs/ 严肃的
    • The teacher looks serious but is actually nice.
  8. lazy /ˈleɪzi/ 懒惰的
    • My lazy brother never cleans his room.
  9. hardworking /ˌhɑːrdˈwɜːrkɪŋ/ 勤奋的
    • She’s hardworking and studies every night.
  10. smart /smɑːrt/ 聪明的
    • The smart student solved the math problem quickly.

4. Clothing Style (穿衣风格)

  1. casual /ˈkæʒuəl/ 休闲的
    • He wears casual clothes like jeans and T-shirts.
  2. formal /ˈfɔːrml/ 正式的
    • We need formal suits for the wedding.
  3. elegant /ˈelɪɡənt/ 优雅的
    • Her elegant dress cost $500.
  4. sporty /ˈspɔːrti/ 运动的
    • He always wears sporty sneakers.
  5. fashionable /ˈfæʃnəbl/ 时尚的
    • Her fashionable bag is from Paris.

5. Other Useful Words

  1. beard /bɪərd/ 胡须
    • He grew a beard during his vacation.
  2. mustache /ˈmʌstæʃ/ 小胡子
    • His mustache looks like a movie villain’s.
  3. tattoo /tæˈtuː/ 纹身
    • She has a tattoo of a rose on her arm.
  4. piercing /ˈpɪəsɪŋ/ 穿孔
    • His ear piercing looks cool.
  5. wrinkles /ˈrɪŋklz/ 皱纹
    • Grandma’s face has gentle wrinkles.
