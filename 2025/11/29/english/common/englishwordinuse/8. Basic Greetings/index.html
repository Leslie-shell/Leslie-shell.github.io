English Word in use （Basic Greetings） --elementray

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， WordInUse 阅读次数：
本文字数： 3.6k 阅读时长 ≈ 3 分钟

1. Basic Greetings (基本问候)

  1. Hello! /həˈloʊ/ 你好！
    • Hello! How are you today?
  2. Hi! /haɪ/ 嗨！
    • Hi! Nice to see you!
  3. Good morning! /ɡʊd ˈmɔːrnɪŋ/ 早上好！
    • Good morning! Did you sleep well?
  4. Good afternoon! /ɡʊd ˌæftərˈnuːn/ 下午好！
    • Good afternoon! It’s sunny today.
  5. Good evening! /ɡʊd ˈiːvnɪŋ/ 晚上好！
    • Good evening! Are you here for dinner?
  6. Good night! /ɡʊd naɪt/ 晚安！
    • Good night! Sweet dreams!
  7. Hey! /heɪ/ 嘿！（非正式）
    • Hey! Wait for me!

2. Asking About Someone (询问近况)

  1. How are you? /haʊ ɑːr ju/ 你好吗？
    • How are you? I haven’t seen you in ages!
  2. How’s it going? /haʊz ɪt ˈɡoʊɪŋ/ 最近怎么样？（非正式）
    • Hey Mark! How’s it going?
  3. How are things? /haʊ ɑːr θɪŋz/ 情况如何？
    • How are things at work?
  4. What’s up? /wʌts ʌp/ 怎么了？（非常非正式）
    • What’s up? You look tired.
  5. How have you been? /haʊ hæv ju bɪn/ 你最近好吗？
    • Long time no see! How have you been?

3. Responding to Greetings (回应问候)

  1. I’m fine, thanks. /aɪm faɪn θæŋks/ 我很好，谢谢。
    • “How are you?” “I’m fine, thanks!”
  2. Not bad. /nɒt bæd/ 还不错。
    • “How’s work?” “Not bad!”
  3. Great! /ɡreɪt/ 很棒！
    • “How are you?” “Great! I just got a new job!”
  4. Could be better. /kʊd bi ˈbetər/ 还行吧（不太理想）。
    • “How are you feeling?” “Could be better—I have a cold.”
  5. Same as usual. /seɪm əz ˈjuːʒuəl/ 老样子。
    • “How’s life?” “Same as usual.”

4. Introductions (介绍)

  1. My name is… /maɪ neɪm ɪz/ 我的名字是…
    • My name is Sarah. Nice to meet you!
  2. What’s your name? /wʌts jɔːr neɪm/ 你叫什么名字？
    • Hi! What’s your name?
  3. This is… /ðɪs ɪz/ 这是…（介绍他人）
    • This is my friend, Tom.
  4. Nice to meet you! /naɪs tə miːt ju/ 很高兴认识你！
    • “I’m Alex.” “Nice to meet you, Alex!”
  5. Pleased to meet you. /pliːzd tə miːt ju/ 幸会。（正式）
    • “I’m Dr. Lee.” “Pleased to meet you.”

5. Saying Goodbye (道别)

  1. Goodbye! /ɡʊdˈbaɪ/ 再见！
    • Goodbye! See you next week!
  2. Bye! /baɪ/ 拜拜！（非正式）
    • Bye! Take care!
  3. See you later! /siː ju ˈleɪtər/ 待会儿见！
    • See you later at the café!
  4. See you soon! /siː ju suːn/ 很快见！
    • See you soon! Don’t be late!
  5. Take care! /teɪk keər/ 保重！
    • Bye, Mom! Take care!
  6. Have a nice day! /hæv ə naɪs deɪ/ 祝你今天愉快！
    • Thanks! Have a nice day too!

6. Wishes & Polite Expressions (祝愿与礼貌用语)

  1. Happy birthday! /ˈhæpi ˈbɜːrθdeɪ/ 生日快乐！
    • Happy birthday! Here’s your gift!
  2. Merry Christmas! /ˈmeri ˈkrɪsməs/ 圣诞快乐！
    • Merry Christmas! Enjoy the holidays!
  3. Happy New Year! /ˈhæpi nuː jɪər/ 新年快乐！
    • Happy New Year! See you in 2025!
  4. Good luck! /ɡʊd lʌk/ 祝你好运！
    • Good luck on your exam tomorrow!
  5. Congratulations! /kənˌɡrætʃəˈleɪʃənz/ 恭喜！
    • Congratulations on your new job!
  6. Get well soon! /ɡet wel suːn/ 早日康复！
    • Sorry you’re sick. Get well soon!
  7. Enjoy your meal! /ɪnˈdʒɔɪ jɔːr miːl/ 请慢用！
    • Here’s your pasta. Enjoy your meal!
  8. Bon appétit! /ˌbɒn æpəˈtiː/ 用餐愉快！（法语常用）
    • Bon appétit! The food looks delicious.

7. Special Occasions (特殊场合)

  1. Happy anniversary! /ˈhæpi ˌænɪˈvɜːrsəri/ 周年纪念快乐！
    • Happy anniversary to the happy couple!
  2. Happy Valentine’s Day! /ˈhæpi ˈvæləntaɪnz deɪ/ 情人节快乐！
    • Happy Valentine’s Day, sweetheart!
  3. Happy Easter! /ˈhæpi ˈiːstər/ 复活节快乐！
    • Happy Easter! Let’s hunt for eggs!
  4. Happy holidays! /ˈhæpi ˈhɒlədeɪz/ 节日快乐！
    • Happy holidays! See you next year!

8. Travel & Farewells (旅行与告别)

  1. Have a safe trip! /hæv ə seɪf trɪp/ 一路平安！
    • Have a safe trip to Japan!
  2. Welcome back! /ˈwelkəm bæk/ 欢迎回来！
    • Welcome back! How was your vacation?
  3. Safe travels! /seɪf ˈtrævəlz/ 旅途平安！
    • Safe travels! Text me when you arrive.
  4. Until next time! /ənˈtɪl nekst taɪm/ 下次见！
    • It was fun! Until next time!

9. Phone & Digital Greetings (电话与数字问候)

  1. Hello? (answering phone) /həˈloʊ/ 喂？
    • “Hello?” “Hi, it’s me!”
  2. Hi there! (online) /haɪ ðeər/ 你好呀！（非正式）
    • Hi there! Thanks for the message.
  3. Good to hear from you! /ɡʊd tə hɪər frəm ju/ 很高兴收到你的消息！
    • Good to hear from you! How’s life?
  4. Talk to you soon! /tɔːk tə ju suːn/ 再聊！
    • Gotta go. Talk to you soon!

10. Cultural Notes (文化小贴士)

  • “How do you do?” is very formal and rarely used today.

  • “What’s up?” is casual—don’t use it with your boss!

  • In the UK, “You alright?” means “How are you?”
