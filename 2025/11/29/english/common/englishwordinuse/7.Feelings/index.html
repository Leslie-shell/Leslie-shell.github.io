Basic Emotions (基础情绪)

  1. happy /ˈhæpi/ 开心的
    • She was happy when she got a puppy.
  2. sad /sæd/ 悲伤的
    • He felt sad after watching the sad movie.
  3. angry /ˈæŋɡri/ 生气的
    • Mom got angry when I broke the vase.
  4. excited /ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/ 兴奋的
    • The kids were excited about the school trip.
  5. bored /bɔːrd/ 无聊的
    • I’m bored—let’s play a game!
  6. surprised /sərˈpraɪzd/ 惊讶的
    • She was surprised by the birthday party.
  7. scared /skeərd/ 害怕的
    • He’s scared of spiders.
  8. tired /ˈtaɪərd/ 疲惫的
    • I’m too tired to cook tonight.
  9. worried /ˈwɜːrid/ 担心的
    • She’s worried about her exam.
  10. confused /kənˈfjuːzd/ 困惑的
    • The instructions confused me.

2. Positive Feelings (积极情绪)

  1. joyful /ˈdʒɔɪfl/ 快乐的
    • The wedding was a joyful event.
  2. proud /praʊd/ 自豪的
    • She felt proud of her son’s achievement.
  3. grateful /ˈɡreɪtfl/ 感激的
    • I’m grateful for your help.
  4. hopeful /ˈhoʊpfl/ 充满希望的
    • We’re hopeful about the future.
  5. relieved /rɪˈliːvd/ 宽慰的
    • He was relieved to hear the good news.
  6. content /kənˈtent/ 满足的
    • She sat by the fire, feeling content.
  7. cheerful /ˈtʃɪəfl/ 愉快的
    • His cheerful smile brightened the room.
  8. loved /lʌvd/ 被爱的
    • Children need to feel loved.
  9. enthusiastic /ɪnˌθuːziˈæstɪk/ 热情的
    • She’s enthusiastic about her new job.
  10. optimistic /ˌɒptɪˈmɪstɪk/ 乐观的
    • He’s optimistic about the weather tomorrow.

3. Negative Feelings (消极情绪)

  1. lonely /ˈloʊnli/ 孤独的
    • She felt lonely after moving to a new city.
  2. frustrated /frʌˈstreɪtɪd/ 沮丧的
    • He got frustrated with the difficult puzzle.
  3. guilty /ˈɡɪlti/ 内疚的
    • I felt guilty for forgetting her birthday.
  4. jealous /ˈdʒeləs/ 嫉妒的
    • She was jealous of her friend’s new bike.
  5. ashamed /əˈʃeɪmd/ 羞愧的
    • He was ashamed of his mistake.
  6. annoyed /əˈnɔɪd/ 恼怒的
    • I’m annoyed by the loud noise.
  7. disappointed /ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪntɪd/ 失望的
    • They were disappointed with the test results.
  8. stressed /strest/ 有压力的
    • Work makes him feel stressed.
  9. anxious /ˈæŋkʃəs/ 焦虑的
    • She felt anxious before the interview.
  10. embarrassed /ɪmˈbærəst/ 尴尬的
    • He was embarrassed when he fell in public.

4. Physical Feelings (身体感受)

  1. hungry /ˈhʌŋɡri/ 饥饿的
    • I’m hungry—let’s eat dinner.
  2. thirsty /ˈθɜːrsti/ 口渴的
    • Drink water if you’re thirsty.
  3. hot /hɒt/ 热的
    • It’s so hot today—I need a fan.
  4. cold /koʊld/ 冷的
    • She’s cold; give her a blanket.
  5. sick /sɪk/ 生病的
    • He stayed home because he felt sick.
  6. dizzy /ˈdɪzi/ 头晕的
    • Stand up slowly or you’ll feel dizzy.
  7. itchy /ˈɪtʃi/ 发痒的
    • The mosquito bite is itchy.
  8. painful /ˈpeɪnfl/ 疼痛的
    • My knee is painful after the fall.
  9. nauseous /ˈnɔːʃəs/ 恶心的
    • The boat ride made me feel nauseous.
  10. energetic /ˌenərˈdʒetɪk/ 精力充沛的
    • After a good sleep, I feel energetic.

5. Phrases & Expressions (短语与表达)

  1. in a good mood 心情好
    • She’s in a good mood today.
  2. in a bad mood 心情不好
    • Don’t talk to him—he’s in a bad mood.
  3. feel like… 想要…
    • I feel like eating ice cream.
  4. can’t stand… 无法忍受…
    • I can’t stand loud chewing.
  5. look forward to… 期待…
    • I look forward to the weekend.
  6. cheer up! 振作起来！
    • Cheer up! Things will get better.
  7. calm down 冷静下来
    • Calm down and take a deep breath.
  8. burst into tears 突然大哭
    • She burst into tears when she heard the news.
  9. lose temper 发脾气
    • He lost his temper during the argument.
  10. feel blue 感到忧郁
    • She’s feeling blue because of the rain.

6. Intensity & Idioms (程度与习语)

  1. thrilled /θrɪld/ 非常兴奋的
    • I was thrilled to meet my favorite singer.
  2. miserable /ˈmɪzrəbl/ 痛苦的
    • He looked miserable after losing the game.
  3. over the moon 欣喜若狂
    • She was over the moon about her promotion.
  4. down in the dumps 情绪低落
    • He’s been down in the dumps since his pet died.
  5. on cloud nine 极度快乐
    • After the wedding, they were on cloud nine.
  6. heartbroken /ˈhɑːrtbroʊkən/ 心碎的
    • She was heartbroken after the breakup.
  7. fed up 受够了
    • I’m fed up with this rainy weather.
  8. shaken up 受惊的
    • He was shaken up after the accident.
  9. overwhelmed /ˌoʊvərˈwelmɪd/ 不堪重负的
    • She felt overwhelmed with work.
  10. on edge 紧张不安
    • The noise kept me on edge all night.

7. Social Feelings (社交情绪)

  1. shy /ʃaɪ/ 害羞的
    • The shy boy hid behind his mother.
  2. confident /ˈkɒnfɪdənt/ 自信的
    • She spoke in a confident voice.
  3. awkward /ˈɔːkwərd/ 尴尬的
    • The silence made everyone feel awkward.
  4. left out 被冷落
    • He felt left out at the party.
  5. welcome /ˈwelkəm/ 受欢迎的
    • They made us feel welcome.
  6. uncomfortable /ʌnˈkʌmftəbl/ 不自在的
    • I felt uncomfortable in the crowded room.
  7. trusting /ˈtrʌstɪŋ/ 信任的
    • She has a trusting nature.
  8. suspicious /səˈspɪʃəs/ 怀疑的
    • He gave her a suspicious look.
  9. impressed /ɪmˈprest/ 印象深刻的
    • We were impressed by her talent.
  10. disgusted /dɪsˈɡʌstɪd/ 厌恶的
    • She was disgusted by the spoiled food.

8. Complex Feelings (复杂情绪)

  1. nostalgic /nɒˈstældʒɪk/ 怀旧的
    • Old photos make me feel nostalgic.
  2. homesick /ˈhoʊmsɪk/ 想家的
    • She felt homesick during her first week abroad.
  3. conflicted /kənˈflɪktɪd/ 矛盾的
    • I’m conflicted about which job to choose.
  4. resentful /rɪˈzentfl/ 愤恨的
    • He grew resentful of his boss’s criticism.
  5. vulnerable /ˈvʌlnərəbl/ 脆弱的
    • After the loss, she felt vulnerable.
  6. apathetic /ˌæpəˈθetɪk/ 冷漠的
    • He seemed apathetic about the election.
  7. overjoyed /ˌoʊvərˈdʒɔɪd/ 狂喜的
    • They were overjoyed at the birth of their baby.
  8. devastated /ˈdevəsteɪtɪd/ 崩溃的
    • She was devastated by the bad news.
  9. giddy /ˈɡɪdi/ 头晕目眩的
    • The roller coaster made me feel giddy.
  10. serene /səˈriːn/ 平静的
    • The lake at dawn looked serene.
