all the waters on the Earth's surface

1. atmosphere /ˈætməsfɪr/

n — the envelope of gases surrounding the Earth; the mood or feeling of a place
中文释义: 大气；氛围

Examples:

  • The candlelight created a romantic atmosphere in the room.
  • Scientists monitor changes in the Earth’s atmosphere.

Common Phrases:

  • Earth’s atmosphere — 地球大气
  • atmospheric pressure — 大气压力

Synonyms:

  • air
  • environment
  • mood

2. hydrosphere /ˈhaɪdrəsfɪr/

n — all the waters on the Earth’s surface
中文释义: 水圈；水界

Examples:

  • Scientists study how pollution affects the Earth’s hydrosphere.
  • The hydrosphere includes oceans, lakes, rivers, and groundwater.

Common Phrases:

  • Earth’s hydrosphere — 地球水圈
  • water cycle in the hydrosphere — 水圈的水循环

Synonyms:

  • water system
  • aquatic environment

3. oxygen /ˈɑːksɪdʒən/

n — a chemical element essential for respiration
中文释义: 氧气

Examples:

  • Plants produce oxygen through photosynthesis.
  • Humans need oxygen to survive.

Common Phrases:

  • oxygen supply — 氧气供应
  • molecular oxygen — 分子氧

Synonyms:

  • O₂

4. oxide /ˈɑːksaɪd/

n — a chemical compound containing oxygen and another element
中文释义: 氧化物

Examples:

  • Rust is a form of iron oxide.
  • Carbon monoxide is a toxic oxide of carbon.

Common Phrases:

  • metal oxide — 金属氧化物
  • oxide layer — 氧化层

5. carbon dioxide /ˈkɑːrbən daɪˈɑːksaɪd/

n — a colorless gas produced by burning carbon and respiration
中文释义: 二氧化碳

Examples:

  • Humans exhale carbon dioxide when breathing.
  • Carbon dioxide levels contribute to global warming.

Common Phrases:

  • CO₂ emissions — 二氧化碳排放
  • carbon dioxide concentration — 二氧化碳浓度

6. hydrogen /ˈhaɪdrədʒən/

n — the lightest chemical element
中文释义: 氢气

Examples:

  • Hydrogen is the lightest element in the periodic table.
  • Hydrogen can be used as a clean fuel source.

Common Phrases:

  • hydrogen gas — 氢气
  • hydrogen fuel cell — 氢燃料电池

7. core /kɔːr/

n — the central part of the Earth
中文释义: 地核；核心

Examples:

  • The Earth’s core is extremely hot and dense.
  • Scientists study the Earth’s core through seismic waves.

Common Phrases:

  • inner core — 内核
  • core temperature — 核心温度

8. crust /krʌst/

n — the outer layer of the Earth
中文释义: 地壳；外壳

Examples:

  • The Earth’s crust is thinnest under the oceans.
  • Continental crust is thicker than oceanic crust.

Common Phrases:

  • Earth’s crust — 地壳
  • crust layer — 外壳层

9. mantle /ˈmæntl/

n — the layer of Earth between the crust and the core
中文释义: 地幔

Examples:

  • The mantle lies between the Earth’s crust and core.
  • Mantle convection drives plate tectonics.

Common Phrases:

  • upper mantle — 上地幔
  • mantle material — 地幔物质

10. longitude /ˈlɒndʒɪtjuːd/

n — the geographic coordinate specifying east-west position
中文释义: 经度

Examples:

  • The ship’s longitude was carefully recorded in the captain’s log.
  • Longitude helps determine time zones.

Common Phrases:

  • degrees of longitude — 经度度数
  • longitude line — 经线
