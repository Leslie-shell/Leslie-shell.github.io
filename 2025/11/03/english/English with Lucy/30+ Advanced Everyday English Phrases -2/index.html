C1-C2高级动词精讲

Advocate

  • 音标：/ˈæd.və.keɪt/
  • 含义：公开支持或提倡（to publicly support or recommend a particular policy or action）
  • 例句：
    • Many doctors advocate a balanced diet and regular exercise.
    • The organization advocates for stricter environmental regulations.
    • She has long advocated reforming the education system.

Amplify

  • 音标：/ˈæm.plɪ.faɪ/
  • 含义：放大；增强（to make something louder or stronger; to increase the effect or impact）
  • 例句：
    • The speaker used a microphone to amplify his voice.
    • Her passionate speech amplified the crowd’s enthusiasm.
    • Social media can amplify both positive and negative messages.

Articulate

  • 音标：/ɑːˈtɪk.jə.leɪt/
  • 含义：清晰表达；清晰发音（to express or explain something clearly; to pronounce words distinctly）
  • 例句：
    • He struggled to articulate his complex ideas simply.
    • She articulates her words very clearly when teaching.
    • The report fails to articulate a clear strategy.

Comprehend

  • 音标：/ˌkɒm.prɪˈhend/
  • 含义：理解，领会（to understand the meaning or significance of something）
  • 例句：
    • I cannot comprehend why he made that decision.
    • The child is too young to comprehend abstract concepts.
    • It’s important to fully comprehend the terms of the agreement.

Convey

  • 音标：/kənˈveɪ/
  • 含义：传达，表达（to communicate or express something to others）
  • 例句：
    • Her smile conveyed more than words ever could.
    • The painting conveys a sense of deep loneliness.
    • Please convey my apologies to your family.

Corroborate

  • 音标：/kəˈrɒb.ə.reɪt/
  • 含义：证实，支持（to confirm or support a statement with evidence or testimony）
  • 例句：
    • Several witnesses corroborated the victim’s account.
    • New research corroborates the original findings.
    • We need more evidence to corroborate this hypothesis.

Decipher

  • 音标：/dɪˈsaɪ.fər/
  • 含义：破译；辨认（to discover the meaning of something written in a code or difficult way）
  • 例句：
    • Archaeologists are trying to decipher the ancient script.
    • I can’t decipher his messy handwriting.
    • It took experts years to decipher the secret code.

Differentiate

  • 音标：/ˌdɪf.əˈren.ʃi.eɪt/
  • 含义：区分，辨别（to distinguish or recognize differences between things）
  • 例句：
    • It’s important to differentiate between facts and opinions.
    • The training helps doctors differentiate similar symptoms.
    • Can you differentiate these two species of birds?

Dissect

  • 音标：/daɪˈsekt/
  • 含义：剖析；仔细分析（to analyze or examine something in detail by breaking it down）
  • 例句：
    • The professor dissected the poem line by line.
    • We need to dissect the problem to find the root cause.
    • The committee will dissect the proposal in detail.

Efface

  • 音标：/ɪˈfeɪs/
  • 含义：擦掉，抹去（to remove or erase something so it’s no longer visible）
  • 例句：
    • Time had effaced the memories of that day.
    • The vandal tried to efface the historical monument.
    • Nothing could efface the impression she made on me.

Elaborate

  • 音标：/iˈlæb.ə.reɪt/
  • 含义：详尽阐述（to add more detail or explanation to something）
  • 例句：
    • Could you elaborate on your previous statement?
    • She elaborated her plan with detailed diagrams.
    • The chef elaborated a simple recipe into a gourmet dish.

Exacerbate

  • 音标：/ɪɡˈzæs.ə.beɪt/
  • 含义：使恶化，加剧（to make a situation or problem worse）
  • 例句：
    • His criticism only exacerbated the situation.
    • The drought has exacerbated food shortages in the region.
    • Stress can exacerbate existing health problems.

Extrapolate

  • 音标：/ɪkˈstræp.ə.leɪt/
  • 含义：推断，推知（to estimate or forecast something using existing knowledge）
  • 例句：
    • We can extrapolate future trends from current data.
    • It’s dangerous to extrapolate from such a small sample.
    • The scientist extrapolated the results to the wider population.

Facilitate

  • 音标：/fəˈsɪl.ɪ.teɪt/
  • 含义：促进，使便利（to make something possible or easier to do）
  • 例句：
    • The new software will facilitate faster communication.
    • A mediator was brought in to facilitate the discussion.
    • Good infrastructure facilitates economic development.

Galvanize

  • 音标：/ˈɡæl.və.naɪz/
  • 含义：刺激，激励（to shock or excite someone into taking action）
  • 例句：
    • The tragedy galvanized the community into action.
    • Her inspiring speech galvanized the team to work harder.
    • The crisis galvanized public opinion against the government.

Integrate

  • 音标：/ˈɪn.tɪ.ɡreɪt/
  • 含义：整合，使融入（to combine or blend things together harmoniously）
  • 例句：
    • The new students quickly integrated into the school community.
    • We need to integrate these different systems seamlessly.
    • The company aims to integrate sustainability into all operations.

Mitigate

  • 音标：/ˈmɪt.ɪ.ɡeɪt/
  • 含义：减轻，缓解（to make something less harmful or unpleasant）
  • 例句：
    • Planting trees helps mitigate climate change effects.
    • The medicine can mitigate the symptoms but not cure the disease.
    • Safety measures are in place to mitigate potential risks.

Perceive

  • 音标：/pəˈsiːv/
  • 含义：认为；察觉（to think of something in a particular way; to become aware of something）
  • 例句：
    • How do you perceive the current political situation?
    • I perceive a change in his attitude recently.
    • The public perceives the company as environmentally responsible.

Reconcile

  • 音标：/ˈrek.ən.saɪl/
  • 含义：使和解；调和（to make people become friends again; to make conflicting things compatible）
  • 例句：
    • The couple finally reconciled after years of separation.
    • It’s difficult to reconcile his actions with his words.
    • We must find a way to reconcile these conflicting demands.

Scrutinize

  • 音标：/ˈskruː.tɪ.naɪz/
  • 含义：仔细检查，详审（to examine or inspect something very closely and thoroughly）
  • 例句：
    • The committee will scrutinize every aspect of the proposal.
    • Her work was closely scrutinized by experts in the field.
    • Investors are scrutinizing the company’s financial reports.
