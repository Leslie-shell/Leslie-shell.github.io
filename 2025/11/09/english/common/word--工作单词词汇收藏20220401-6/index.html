工作单词词汇收藏20220401-6

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220401 阅读次数：
本文字数： 1.3k 阅读时长 ≈ 1 分钟

1. invader /ɪnˈveɪdər/

n — a person, army, or country that enters another place by force
中文释义: 入侵者；侵略者。

Examples:

  • The invaders were repelled by the local army.
  • Ancient invaders often shaped the culture of the conquered land.

Common phrases:

  • foreign invader — 外来入侵者
  • enemy invader — 敌方入侵者
  • invader forces — 入侵部队

2. fragment /ˈfræɡmənt/

n — a small part broken off or detached from something
中文释义: 碎片；片段。

Examples:

  • The archaeologists found fragments of pottery.
  • Only fragments of the ancient text survived.

Common phrases:

  • fragment of glass — 玻璃碎片
  • broken fragment — 破碎的碎片
  • fragment of memory — 记忆片段

3. victor /ˈvɪktər/

n — a person who wins a contest, battle, or competition
中文释义: 胜利者；获胜者。

Examples:

  • The victor of the election gave a short speech.
  • He emerged as the victor after a long competition.

Common phrases:

  • victorious victor — 胜利者
  • declared victor — 宣布胜利者
  • victorious campaign — 胜利的战役

4. assignment /əˈsaɪnmənt/

n — a task or piece of work given to someone as part of a job or course
中文释义: 作业；任务；分配。

Examples:

  • The teacher gave us a difficult assignment.
  • He completed the assignment ahead of schedule.

Common phrases:

  • homework assignment — 家庭作业
  • job assignment — 工作任务
  • project assignment — 项目任务

5. commercial /kəˈmɜːrʃəl/

adj — connected with business or intended to make a profit
中文释义: 商业的；用于营利的。

Examples:

  • The building is for commercial use.
  • The company produces commercial products for the market.

Common phrases:

  • commercial sector — 商业部门
  • commercial enterprise — 商业企业
  • commercial advertising — 商业广告
