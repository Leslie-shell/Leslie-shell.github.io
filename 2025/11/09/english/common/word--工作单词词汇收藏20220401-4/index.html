工作单词词汇收藏20220401-4

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220401 阅读次数：

1. irrespectively /ˌɪrɪˈspektɪvli/

adv — without considering; regardless of
中文释义: 不管；无论……都……

Examples:

  • Students are treated equally irrespectively of their background.
  • The rules apply irrespectively of age or gender.

Common phrases:

  • (常用短语直接使用 “irrespectively of”)
  • irrespectively of circumstances — 不论情况如何

2. viciously /ˈvɪʃəsli/

adv — in a violent or cruel way
中文释义: 凶恶地；残忍地

Examples:

  • The dog attacked viciously without warning.
  • He was viciously criticized for his comments.

Common phrases:

  • act viciously — 行为凶狠
  • attacked viciously — 凶猛攻击

3. significant /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/

adj — important or large enough to be noticed or have an effect
中文释义: 重要的；显著的

Examples:

  • There was a significant increase in sales this year.
  • This decision will have a significant impact on the company.

Common phrases:

  • significant change — 显著变化
  • significant difference — 显著差异
  • significant impact — 重大影响

4. pitfall /ˈpɪtfɔːl/

n — a hidden danger or difficulty
中文释义: 陷阱；潜在困难

Examples:

  • There are many pitfalls to avoid when starting a new business.
  • One common pitfall is underestimating costs.

Common phrases:

  • avoid pitfalls — 避免陷阱
  • common pitfall — 常见陷阱

5. benchmark /ˈbentʃmɑːk/

n — a standard or point of reference against which things may be compared
中文释义: 基准；参考标准

Examples:

  • The company uses industry benchmarks to measure performance.
  • This project will set a benchmark for future work.

Common phrases:

  • performance benchmark — 绩效基准
  • industry benchmark — 行业标准
  • set a benchmark — 设立基准

6. mandatory /ˈmændətəri/

adj — required by law or rules
中文释义: 强制的；必须的

Examples:

  • Wearing a helmet is mandatory for all construction workers.
  • Attendance at the meeting is mandatory.

Common phrases:

  • mandatory requirement — 强制要求
  • mandatory training — 强制培训
  • mandatory policy — 强制政策

7. investigation /ɪnˌvestɪˈɡeɪʃən/

n — a careful examination or study to find facts
中文释义: 调查；研究

Examples:

  • The police carried out a thorough investigation.
  • The investigation revealed several safety issues.

Common phrases:

  • conduct an investigation — 进行调查
  • criminal investigation — 刑事调查
  • investigation report — 调查报告

8. overestimate /ˌoʊvərˈestɪmeɪt/

v — to judge something to be larger, better, or more important than it really is
中文释义: 高估；过高估计

Examples:

  • Investors tend to overestimate potential profits.
  • Don’t overestimate your ability to finish the project alone.

Common phrases:

  • overestimate value — 高估价值
  • overestimate risk — 高估风险

9. graphic /ˈɡræfɪk/

adj — relating to visual images or diagrams
中文释义: 图表的；图像的

Examples:

  • The report included graphic illustrations of the data.
  • Graphic design is important for advertising.

Common phrases:

  • graphic representation — 图形表示
  • graphic design — 平面设计
  • graphic content — 图像内容

10. engraving /ɪnˈɡreɪvɪŋ/

n — the art or process of carving words or designs into a surface
中文释义: 雕刻；雕刻术

Examples:

  • The museum displayed ancient stone engravings.
  • He learned engraving as a hobby.

Common phrases:

  • wood engraving — 木刻
  • metal engraving — 金属雕刻
  • engraving tool — 雕刻工具
