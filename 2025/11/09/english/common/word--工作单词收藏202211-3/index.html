adj — becoming worse in quality or condition
中文释义: 恶化的；变坏的。

Examples:

  • The patient’s condition is deteriorating.
  • Relations between the two countries are deteriorating.

Common Phrases:

  • deteriorating health — 健康状况恶化

Synonyms:

Word Root:
