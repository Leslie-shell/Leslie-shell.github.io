11.among | forward |addition |adjustment|advertisement|amount

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220325 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.4k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟

1. among /əˈmʌŋ/

preposition — in the middle of or surrounded by a group of people or things

中文释义: 在……之中；被……围绕。

Examples:

  • She was sitting among her friends at the party.
  • The decision caused disagreement among the team members.

Synonyms: amidst, surrounded by, in the midst of

Common Phrases

  • among friends — 在朋友之间
    • He felt relaxed among friends.
  • divide among — 在……之间分配
    • The money was divided among the children.
  • popular among — 在……中受欢迎
    • This song is popular among teenagers.

2. forward /ˈfɔːrwərd/

adverb — toward a place, time, or position that is in front

中文释义: 向前；朝前。

Examples:

  • She took a step forward and smiled.
  • Looking forward, we expect sales to grow next year.

Synonyms: ahead, onward, frontwards

Common Phrases

  • look forward to — 期待
    • I’m looking forward to the weekend.
  • move forward — 前进；推进
    • Let’s move forward with the plan.
  • step forward — 挺身而出
    • Several volunteers stepped forward to help.

3. addition /əˈdɪʃən/

noun — the process of adding something to something else

中文释义: 添加；增加。

Examples:

  • The addition of new members strengthened the team.
  • Sugar is a common addition to coffee.

Synonyms: increase, supplement, increment

Common Phrases

  • in addition to — 除……之外
    • In addition to English, he speaks French.
  • an addition to — ……的补充
    • The new library is a welcome addition to the school.
  • make an addition — 做出增加
    • We made an addition to the list.

4. adjustment /əˈdʒʌstmənt/

noun — a small change made to improve or correct something

中文释义: 调整；改动。

Examples:

  • The camera needs a slight adjustment.
  • She made a few adjustments to her schedule.

Synonyms: modification, alteration, adaptation

Common Phrases

  • make an adjustment — 做出调整
    • We had to make an adjustment to the plan.
  • price adjustment — 价格调整
    • The store announced a price adjustment on electronics.
  • emotional adjustment — 情感适应
    • Moving to a new city requires emotional adjustment.

5. advertisement /ədˈvɜːrtɪsmənt/

noun — a notice, picture, or short film promoting a product, service, or event

中文释义: 广告；宣传。

Examples:

  • I saw an advertisement for a new phone on TV.
  • The company placed an advertisement in the local newspaper.

Synonyms: commercial, promotion, publicity

Common Phrases

  • TV advertisement — 电视广告
    • The TV advertisement was very catchy.
  • online advertisement — 网络广告
    • Online advertisements target specific audiences.
  • job advertisement — 招聘广告
    • He applied for a position he saw in a job advertisement.

6. amount /əˈmaʊnt/

noun — a quantity of something, especially that can be measured or counted

中文释义: 数量；总额。

Examples:

  • He spent a large amount of money on the trip.
  • The total amount due is $500.

Synonyms: quantity, sum, total

Common Phrases

  • a large/small amount of — 大量的／少量的
    • She drinks a large amount of water every day.
  • the amount of time — 时间量
    • The amount of time required depends on the task.
  • total amount — 总金额
    • Please check the total amount before paying.
