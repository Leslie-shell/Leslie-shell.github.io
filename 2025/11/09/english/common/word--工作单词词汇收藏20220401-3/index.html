工作单词词汇收藏20220401-3

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220401 阅读次数：
本文字数： 3.2k 阅读时长 ≈ 3 分钟

1. bottleneck /ˈbɑːtlnek/

n — a point in a process where progress is limited or slowed
中文释义: 瓶颈；限制进程或效率的障碍。

Examples:

  • The slow database is a bottleneck in the system.
  • Identifying bottlenecks is essential for process improvement.

Common phrases:

  • remove a bottleneck — 消除瓶颈
  • performance bottleneck — 性能瓶颈
  • production bottleneck — 生产瓶颈

Idioms:

  • hit a bottleneck — 遇到瓶颈
    • Example: Our project hit a bottleneck when the server crashed.

2. phase /feɪz/

n — a stage in a process of change or development
中文释义: 阶段；时期。

Examples:

  • We are entering the final phase of the project.
  • The training program is divided into three phases.

Common phrases:

  • initial phase — 初始阶段
  • final phase — 最终阶段

Idioms:

  • out of phase — 不协调，不同步
    • Example: The two machines are out of phase, causing vibrations.

3. optimize /ˈɒptɪmaɪz/

v — to make something as good or effective as possible
中文释义: 优化；使达到最佳状态。

Examples:

  • We need to optimize our code for better performance.
  • Marketing strategies were optimized to reach a wider audience.

Common phrases:

  • optimize performance — 优化性能
  • optimize resources — 优化资源
  • optimize process — 优化流程

4. concentrate /ˈkɒnsəntreɪt/

v — to give all your attention to something
中文释义: 集中；专注。

Examples:

  • Please concentrate on your studies.
  • He concentrated all his efforts on completing the project.

Common phrases:

  • concentrate on — 专注于
  • concentrate resources — 集中资源
  • highly concentrated — 高度集中的

5. implication /ˌɪmplɪˈkeɪʃən/

n — a possible effect or result of an action or decision
中文释义: 含义；影响；可能的后果。

Examples:

  • The new law has significant implications for businesses.
  • His words carried deep political implications.

Common phrases:

  • practical implication — 实际意义
  • legal implication — 法律影响
  • implication for — 对……的影响

6. scale up /skeɪl ʌp/

v — to increase in size, amount, or production
中文释义: 扩大规模；增加数量或能力。

Examples:

  • The company plans to scale up production next year.
  • Startups often struggle to scale up efficiently.

Common phrases:

  • scale up operations — 扩大运营
  • scale up production — 扩大生产
  • scale up quickly — 快速扩张

7. explicitly /ɪkˈsplɪsɪtli/

adv — in a clear and detailed way, leaving no room for confusion or doubt
中文释义: 明确地；清楚地。

Examples:

  • The teacher explicitly explained the rules.
  • Instructions were explicitly stated in the manual.

Common phrases:

  • explicitly mention — 明确提到
  • explicitly stated — 明确说明
  • explicitly require — 明确要求

8. specific /spəˈsɪfɪk/

adj — clearly defined or identified
中文释义: 具体的；特定的。

Examples:

  • Can you give a specific example?
  • The software is designed for a specific task.

Common phrases:

  • specific purpose — 特定用途
  • specific requirement — 具体要求
  • specific example — 具体例子

9. drastically /ˈdræstɪkli/

adv — in a way that is severe or sudden
中文释义: 激烈地；大幅度地。

Examples:

  • The policy drastically changed the industry.
  • He cut costs drastically to save the company.

Common phrases:

  • change drastically — 大幅改变
  • decrease drastically — 大幅下降
  • increase drastically — 大幅增加

10. be fault-tolerant /bi ˈfɔːltˌtɒlərənt/

adj — able to continue functioning even if parts fail
中文释义: 容错的；能承受错误或故障的。

Examples:

  • The system is designed to be fault-tolerant.
  • Fault-tolerant architectures prevent complete failures.

Common phrases:

  • fault-tolerant system — 容错系统
  • highly fault-tolerant — 高度容错
  • design for fault-tolerance — 为容错设计

11. chuffed /tʃʌft/

adj — very pleased or happy
中文释义: 非常高兴；非常满足。

Examples:

  • She was chuffed to receive the award for her work.
  • I’m really chuffed with how the project turned out.

Common phrases:

  • be chuffed about — 对……感到高兴
  • be chuffed with — 对……感到满意

12. skive /skaɪv/

v — to avoid work or school by staying away or leaving early
中文释义: 逃避工作或逃学；旷课。

Examples:

  • He skived off school to go to the football match.
  • She tends to skive when the workload gets too heavy.

Common phrases:

  • skive off — 逃避（工作/学校）
  • skive from class — 逃课
