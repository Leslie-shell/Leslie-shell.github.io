工作单词词汇收藏20220401-1

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220401 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.7k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟

1. performance /pərˈfɔːrməns/

n — the act of performing a task, work, or show; the quality of doing something
中文释义: 表演；表现；性能；业绩。

Examples:

  • His performance in the exam was excellent.
  • The car’s performance improved after the upgrade.

Common phrases:

  • job performance — 工作表现
  • high performance — 高性能
  • performance review — 绩效评估

2. optimization /ˌɒptɪmaɪˈzeɪʃən/

n — the process of making something as effective or functional as possible
中文释义: 优化；最佳化。

Examples:

  • Website optimization can greatly improve loading speed.
  • They focus on optimization of the supply chain.

Common phrases:

  • code optimization — 代码优化
  • performance optimization — 性能优化
  • optimization algorithm — 优化算法

3. concurrency /kənˈkʌrənsi/

n — the occurrence of multiple events or processes at the same time
中文释义: 并发；同时发生。

Examples:

  • Concurrency is a key concept in modern programming.
  • The system supports high concurrency for many users.

Common phrases:

  • concurrent processing — 并发处理
  • concurrency control — 并发控制
  • concurrency issue — 并发问题

4. circumstance /ˈsɜːrkəmstæns/

n — the conditions or facts affecting a situation
中文释义: 环境；情况；情形。

Examples:

  • Under no circumstance should you open that door.
  • The decision depends on the circumstance.

Common phrases:

  • under the circumstance — 在这种情况下
  • special circumstance — 特殊情况
  • in any circumstance — 无论如何

5. maximum /ˈmæksɪməm/

n/adj — the greatest amount, value, or degree possible
中文释义: 最大值；最大的。

Examples:

  • The maximum speed of this car is 220 km/h.
  • Please set the volume to the maximum.

Common phrases:

  • maximum value — 最大值
  • maximum effort — 最大努力
  • to the maximum — 尽最大程度

6. enterprise /ˈentərpraɪz/

n — a business organization; initiative or willingness to take risks
中文释义: 企业；事业；进取心。

Examples:

  • He runs a large construction enterprise.
  • Small enterprises often innovate rapidly.

Common phrases:

  • private enterprise — 私营企业
  • social enterprise — 社会企业
  • enterprise management — 企业管理

7. reserve /rɪˈzɜːrv/

v/n — to keep something for future use; a supply stored for later
中文释义: 预定；保留；储备。

Examples:

  • I’d like to reserve a table for two.
  • The country has a reserve of foreign currency.

Common phrases:

  • reserve a seat — 预订座位
  • in reserve — 储备
  • nature reserve — 自然保护区

8. reproduce /ˌriːprəˈduːs/

v — to make a copy of something; to generate offspring
中文释义: 复制；繁殖；再现。

Examples:

  • The printer can reproduce images in high quality.
  • Many species reproduce in spring.

Common phrases:

  • reproduce sound — 重现声音
  • reproduce art — 复制艺术品
  • reproduce exactly — 精确再现

9. innovate /ˈɪnəveɪt/

v — to introduce new ideas, methods, or products
中文释义: 创新；改革；引入新方法。

Examples:

  • Companies need to innovate to stay competitive in the market.
  • The school tries to innovate its teaching methods every year.

Common phrases:

  • innovate in technology — 在技术上创新
  • ability to innovate — 创新能力
  • innovate products — 创新产品

10. transmit /trænzˈmɪt/

v — to send or pass something from one person or place to another
中文释义: 传送；传递；传播（信息、信号、疾病等）。

Examples:

  • The radio station can transmit signals across the country.
  • Diseases can be transmitted through close contact.

Common phrases:

  • transmit information — 传递信息
  • transmit data — 传输数据
  • transmit a message — 传达信息
