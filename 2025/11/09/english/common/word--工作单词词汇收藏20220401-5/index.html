工作单词词汇收藏20220401-5

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220401 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.4k 阅读时长 ≈ 2 分钟

1. established /ɪˈstæblɪʃt/

adj — recognized or accepted over a long period; having existed for a long time
中文释义: 已建立的；公认的；已有声望的。

Examples:

  • She works for an established law firm.
  • The restaurant is one of the most established in the city.

Common phrases:

  • established company — 老牌公司
  • established procedure — 既定程序
  • well-established — 信誉良好的

2. charming /ˈtʃɑːrmɪŋ/

adj — very pleasant or attractive
中文释义: 迷人的；有吸引力的。

Examples:

  • She gave a charming smile.
  • The village is charming and picturesque.

Common phrases:

  • charming personality — 迷人的个性
  • charming smile — 迷人的微笑
  • utterly charming — 非常迷人

3. keen /kiːn/

adj — very enthusiastic or interested
中文释义: 热衷的；渴望的；敏锐的。

Examples:

  • He is keen on learning new languages.
  • She has a keen interest in classical music.

Common phrases:

  • keen interest — 强烈兴趣
  • keen observer — 敏锐的观察者
  • keen sense — 敏锐感知

4. draw up /drɔː ʌp/

phrasal v — to prepare something in writing, especially a plan, contract, or list
中文释义: 起草；拟定（计划、合同等）。

Examples:

  • They drew up a new contract for the employees.
  • The committee drew up a list of recommendations.

Common phrases:

  • draw up a plan — 起草计划
  • draw up a contract — 拟定合同
  • draw up a report — 起草报告

5. frustrated /ˈfrʌstreɪtɪd/

adj — feeling annoyed or disappointed because of inability to achieve something
中文释义: 挫败的；沮丧的。

Examples:

  • He felt frustrated after failing the exam.
  • She was frustrated by the slow progress of the project.

Common phrases:

  • frustrated attempt — 挫败的尝试
  • frustrated employee — 感到挫败的员工
  • frustrated ambitions — 受挫的抱负

6. horizons /həˈraɪzənz/

n — the limits of a person’s knowledge, experience, or interest
中文释义: 视野；眼界；范围。

Examples:

  • Traveling abroad broadens your horizons.
  • She is always eager to expand her horizons.

Common phrases:

  • broaden your horizons — 拓宽视野
  • new horizons — 新领域
  • expand horizons — 扩展眼界

7. diplomacy /dɪˈpləʊməsi/

n — the art of managing negotiations and relationships between countries or people
中文释义: 外交；策略；交际手腕。

Examples:

  • Diplomacy is crucial in international relations.
  • He handled the negotiations with great diplomacy.

Common phrases:

  • international diplomacy — 国际外交
  • skillful diplomacy — 高超的外交手腕
  • practice diplomacy — 运用外交策略

8. unify /ˈjuːnɪfaɪ/

v — to make people, groups, or countries come together as one
中文释义: 统一；使联合。

Examples:

  • The leader tried to unify the different factions.
  • Efforts were made to unify the country’s education system.

Common phrases:

  • unify forces — 联合力量
  • unify policies — 统一政策
  • unify a team — 团队统一

9. commanding /kəˈmændɪŋ/

adj — having authority or control; impressive
中文释义: 威严的；指挥的；令人印象深刻的。

Examples:

  • He has a commanding presence in the room.
  • The general took a commanding view of the battlefield.

Common phrases:

  • commanding officer — 指挥官
  • commanding view — 宏伟视野
  • commanding position — 主导地位
