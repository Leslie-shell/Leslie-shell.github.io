impulse | vital | eliminate | ubiquitous | cumbersome | redundancy

1. impulse /ˈɪmpʌls/

n — a sudden strong desire or urge to act
中文释义: 冲动；推动力。

Examples:

  • He acted on an impulse and bought the expensive watch.
  • The impulse to help others is natural in humans.

Common Phrases:

  • sudden impulse — 突然的冲动
  • impulse buying — 冲动购物
  • electrical impulse — 电冲动

Synonyms:

  • urge
  • drive
  • instinct

Word Root:

  • Root: impulsus (Latin: “to push against”) → 与推动、冲动相关
  • Related Words:
    • impulsive — 冲动的
    • impulsion — 推动力

2. vital /ˈvaɪtl/

adj — absolutely necessary or important; full of life
中文释义: 至关重要的；充满活力的。

Examples:

  • Water is vital for all living beings.
  • She plays a vital role in the team.

Common Phrases:

  • vital signs — 生命体征
  • vital information — 重要信息
  • vital organ — 重要器官

Synonyms:

  • essential
  • crucial
  • critical

Word Root:

  • Root: vitalis (Latin: “of life”) → 与生命、重要性相关
  • Related Words:
    • vitality — 活力
    • revitalize — 使恢复生机

3. eliminate /ɪˈlɪmɪneɪt/

v — to remove or get rid of something
中文释义: 消除；排除。

Examples:

  • They want to eliminate errors in the system.
  • The company eliminated several outdated positions.

Common Phrases:

  • eliminate risk — 消除风险
  • eliminate doubt — 消除疑虑
  • eliminate competition — 淘汰竞争

Synonyms:

  • remove
  • eradicate
  • abolish

Word Root:

  • Root: eliminare (Latin: “to turn out of doors”) → 与去除、排除相关
  • Related Words:
    • elimination — 消除
    • eliminator — 消除者

4. ubiquitous /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/

adj — present or found everywhere
中文释义: 无处不在的。

Examples:

  • Smartphones are ubiquitous in modern society.
  • Fast-food chains have become ubiquitous worldwide.

Common Phrases:

  • ubiquitous presence — 无处不在
  • ubiquitous technology — 普及技术
  • ubiquitous advertising — 无处不在的广告

Synonyms:

  • omnipresent
  • pervasive
  • universal

Word Root:

  • Root: ubique (Latin: “everywhere”) → 与无处不在相关
  • Related Words:
    • ubiquity — 无处不在
    • ubiquitously — 无处不在地

5. cumbersome /ˈkʌmbərsəm/

adj — large or heavy and therefore difficult to carry or use
中文释义: 笨重的；累赘的。

Examples:

  • The old computer system was cumbersome and slow.
  • Carrying the luggage was cumbersome.

Common Phrases:

  • cumbersome process — 繁琐过程
  • cumbersome equipment — 笨重设备
  • cumbersome rules — 繁琐规则

Synonyms:

  • unwieldy
  • bulky
  • clumsy

Word Root:

  • Root: cumbrare (Latin: “to hinder”) → 与阻碍、笨重相关
  • Related Words:
    • cumbersomely — 笨重地
    • cumbersomeness — 笨重

6. redundancy /rɪˈdʌndənsi/

n — the state of being not necessary because something else exists
中文释义: 冗余；多余。

Examples:

  • There is a lot of redundancy in the report.
  • Eliminating redundancy can improve efficiency.

Common Phrases:

  • redundancy in text — 文本冗余
  • job redundancy — 职位冗余
  • redundant system — 冗余系统

Synonyms:

  • superfluity
  • repetition
  • excess

Word Root:

  • Root: redundantia (Latin: “overflow, excess”) → 与多余、重复相关
  • Related Words:
    • redundant — 多余的
    • redundantly — 冗余地
