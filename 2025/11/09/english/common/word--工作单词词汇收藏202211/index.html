1. material /məˈtɪəriəl/

n — the matter from which a thing is or can be made
中文释义: 材料；物质。

Examples:

  • The table is made of high-quality material.
  • Teachers provide study material for students.

Common Phrases:

  • raw material — 原材料
  • teaching material — 教学材料
  • material goods — 物质财富

Synonyms:

  • substance
  • matter
  • fabric

Word Root:

  • Root: materia (Latin: “matter, substance”) → 与物质、材料相关
  • Related Words:
    • materialize — 实现
    • materialistic — 唯物的

2. operation /ˌɒpəˈreɪʃn/

n — the process of functioning or working; a medical procedure
中文释义: 运作；手术。

Examples:

  • The factory operation runs 24 hours a day.
  • He is recovering after a heart operation.

Common Phrases:

  • business operation — 企业运作
  • military operation — 军事行动
  • surgical operation — 外科手术

Synonyms:

  • procedure
  • function
  • process

Word Root:

  • Root: operari (Latin: “to work”) → 与工作、操作相关
  • Related Words:
    • operate — 操作，运作
    • operator — 操作者

3. melody /ˈmelədi/

n — a sequence of musical notes forming a tune
中文释义: 旋律；曲调。

Examples:

  • The song has a beautiful melody.
  • She hummed a familiar melody.

Common Phrases:

  • catchy melody — 上口旋律
  • instrumental melody — 器乐旋律
  • vocal melody — 人声旋律

Synonyms:

  • tune
  • air
  • song

Word Root:

  • Root: melodia (Latin: “singing, chant”) → 与音乐、旋律相关
  • Related Words:
    • melodic — 悦耳的
    • melodiously — 悦耳地

4. specification /ˌspesɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/

n — a detailed description of the design and materials used to make something
中文释义: 规格说明；详细说明。

Examples:

  • The product meets all safety specifications.
  • The engineers followed the technical specification strictly.

Common Phrases:

  • product specification — 产品规格
  • technical specification — 技术说明
  • design specification — 设计规范

Synonyms:

  • requirement
  • description
  • standard

Word Root:

  • Root: specificare (Latin: “to make specific”) → 与明确、说明相关
  • Related Words:
    • specify — 指定，说明
    • specific — 具体的

5. slap /slæp/

v — to hit someone or something with an open hand
中文释义: 拍打；掴。

Examples:

  • He slapped the table in anger.
  • She was slapped for being late.

Common Phrases:

  • slap in the face — 打脸
  • slap on the wrist — 轻微惩罚
  • slap together — 匆忙做

Synonyms:

  • hit
  • strike
  • smack

Word Root:

  • Root: 源自拟声词 “slap” → 与拍击相关
  • Related Words:
    • slapping — 拍打的
    • slapdash — 草率的

6. predict /prɪˈdɪkt/

v — to say what will happen in the future
中文释义: 预测；预言。

Examples:

  • Experts predict a rise in inflation.
  • It’s difficult to predict the outcome of the game.

Common Phrases:

  • predict the future — 预测未来
  • accurately predict — 精确预测
  • predict trends — 预测趋势

Synonyms:

  • forecast
  • anticipate
  • foretell

Word Root:

  • Root: praedicere (Latin: “to proclaim beforehand”) → 与预言相关
  • Related Words:
    • prediction — 预测
    • predictable — 可预测的

7. relativity /ˌreləˈtɪvɪti/

n — the state of being relative; Einstein’s theory of relativity
中文释义: 相对性；相对论。

Examples:

  • The theory of relativity changed physics.
  • Time is a concept studied in relativity.

Common Phrases:

  • theory of relativity — 相对论
  • special relativity — 狭义相对论
  • general relativity — 广义相对论

Synonyms:

  • relativeness
  • dependence

Word Root:

  • Root: relativus (Latin: “having reference to”) → 与相对、关系相关
  • Related Words:
    • relative — 相对的
    • relate — 关联

8. evolution /ˌiːvəˈluːʃn/

n — the gradual development of something
中文释义: 演变；进化。

Examples:

  • Evolution has shaped modern species.
  • The evolution of technology is rapid.

Common Phrases:

  • theory of evolution — 进化论
  • gradual evolution — 逐步演变
  • cultural evolution — 文化演变

Synonyms:

  • development
  • growth
  • progression

Word Root:

  • Root: evolutio (Latin: “unrolling”) → 与发展、展开相关
  • Related Words:
    • evolve — 发展，进化
    • evolutionary — 进化的

9. scarce /skeəs/

adj — insufficient for the demand; rare
中文释义: 缺乏的；稀少的。

Examples:

  • Food was scarce during the famine.
  • Jobs are scarce in the rural area.

Common Phrases:

  • scarce resources — 稀缺资源
  • scarce supply — 供应不足
  • become scarce — 变得稀少

Synonyms:

  • rare
  • limited
  • insufficient

Word Root:

  • Root: scarcius (Old French/Latin: “scant, rare”) → 与稀少相关
  • Related Words:
    • scarceness — 稀缺
    • scarcely — 几乎不

10. hyper /ˈhaɪpər/

prefix — over, excessive, beyond
中文释义: 超过；过度。

Examples:

  • He is hyperactive in class.
  • The internet allows hyperconnectivity among people.

Common Phrases:

  • hyperactive — 活动过度的
  • hypertension — 高血压
  • hyperbole — 夸张

Synonyms:

  • over-
  • excessive
  • super-

Word Root:

  • Root: hyper (Greek: “over, beyond”) → 与过度、超过相关
  • Related Words:
    • hyperlink — 超链接
    • hypercritical — 吹毛求疵的
