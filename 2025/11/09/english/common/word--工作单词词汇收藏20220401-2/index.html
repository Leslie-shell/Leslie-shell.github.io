工作单词词汇收藏20220401-2

发表于 2025-11-09 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， 20220401 阅读次数：
本文字数： 2.9k 阅读时长 ≈ 3 分钟

0. prior /ˈpraɪər/

adj — existing or happening before something else
中文释义: 先前的；优先的。

Examples:

  • You need prior experience to apply for this job.
  • Prior to the meeting, she reviewed all the documents.

Common phrases:

  • prior knowledge — 先前知识
  • prior arrangement — 事先安排
  • prior approval — 事先批准

1. embedded /ɪmˈbɛdɪd/

adj — fixed firmly and deeply in a surrounding mass
中文释义: 嵌入的；深深植入的。

Examples:

  • The sensor is embedded in the machine for better accuracy.
  • Cultural values are deeply embedded in society.

Common phrases:

  • embedded system — 嵌入式系统
  • deeply embedded — 深深植入
  • embedded software — 嵌入式软件

2. accuracy /ˈækjərəsi/

n — the quality of being exact or correct
中文释义: 精确性；准确度。

Examples:

  • The accuracy of the measurements is critical in this experiment.
  • GPS devices provide high accuracy in navigation.

Common phrases:

  • high accuracy — 高精度
  • accuracy rate — 准确率
  • measurement accuracy — 测量精度

3. simplicity /sɪmˈplɪsəti/

n — the quality of being easy to understand or do
中文释义: 简单；简易；朴素。

Examples:

  • The simplicity of the design makes it elegant.
  • She admired the simplicity of his solution.

Common phrases:

  • elegant simplicity — 高雅的简洁
  • simplicity in design — 设计上的简洁
  • simplicity principle — 简单原则

4. abuse /əˈbjuːs/

v/n — to use something wrongly or harmfully
中文释义: 滥用；虐待。

Examples:

  • He was accused of abusing his power.
  • Child abuse is a serious social issue.

Common phrases:

  • substance abuse — 药物滥用
  • verbal abuse — 言语虐待
  • abuse of authority — 滥用职权

5. tragedy /ˈtrædʒədi/

n — a very sad event or situation, often involving death or suffering
中文释义: 悲剧；惨事。

Examples:

  • The play depicts the tragedy of human conflict.
  • The earthquake was a national tragedy.

Common phrases:

  • personal tragedy — 个人悲剧
  • historical tragedy — 历史悲剧
  • tragic event — 悲惨事件

6. flavor /ˈfleɪvər/

n — the taste or quality of something
中文释义: 风味；味道；特色。

Examples:

  • This dish has a strong garlic flavor.
  • Jazz music has its own unique flavor.

Common phrases:

  • rich flavor — 浓郁的味道
  • add flavor — 添加风味
  • distinctive flavor — 独特风味

7. distinctive /dɪˈstɪŋktɪv/

adj — having a quality that makes something easily recognizable
中文释义: 独特的；有特色的。

Examples:

  • She has a distinctive style of writing.
  • The building is known for its distinctive architecture.

Common phrases:

  • distinctive feature — 独特特征
  • distinctive style — 独特风格
  • distinctive aroma — 独特香味

8. characteristic /ˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪk/

n/adj — a feature or quality belonging typically to a person, place, or thing
中文释义: 特征；特色。

Examples:

  • Kindness is a characteristic of her personality.
  • Each culture has its own distinctive characteristics.

Common phrases:

  • defining characteristic — 核心特征
  • characteristic feature — 特征性特征
  • characteristic behavior — 特征性行为

9. instrumentation /ˌɪnstrəmenˈteɪʃən/

n — the devices used for measuring, recording, or controlling something
中文释义: 仪器设备；器械学。

Examples:

  • Modern instrumentation allows precise measurements.
  • The lab is equipped with advanced instrumentation.

Common phrases:

  • medical instrumentation — 医学仪器
  • instrumentation system — 仪器系统
  • scientific instrumentation — 科学仪器

10. metrics /ˈmetrɪks/

n — a set of measurements that evaluate performance or progress
中文释义: 指标；度量标准。

Examples:

  • The company uses various metrics to evaluate employee performance.
  • Website metrics show a significant increase in traffic this month.

Common phrases:

  • performance metrics — 绩效指标
  • key metrics — 关键指标
  • marketing metrics — 营销指标
