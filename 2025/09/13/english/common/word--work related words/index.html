Vocabulary

1. regulator /ˈrɛɡjʊleɪtər/

n — a person or organization that supervises a particular activity or industry
中文释义: 监管者。

Examples:

  • Regulators monitor the banking industry. 监管者监督银行业。
  • The energy regulators set electricity prices. 能源监管者设定电价。

Common phrases:

  • financial regulator — 金融监管者
  • energy regulator — 能源监管者
  • government regulator — 政府监管者

2. economic /ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk/

adj — relating to the economy or finances
中文释义: 经济的。

Examples:

  • Economic growth is a priority for the government. 政府优先考虑经济增长。
  • Economic policies affect employment rates. 经济政策影响就业率。

Common phrases:

  • economic growth — 经济增长
  • economic policy — 经济政策
  • economic trend — 经济趋势

3. macroeconomic /ˌmækrəʊˌiːkəˈnɒmɪks/

n — the branch of economics concerned with large-scale or general economic factors
中文释义: 宏观经济学。

Examples:

  • Macroeconomic indicators show inflation is rising. 宏观经济指标显示通胀在上升。
  • Analysts study macroeconomic trends to forecast growth. 分析师研究宏观经济趋势以预测增长。

Common phrases:

  • macroeconomic policy — 宏观经济政策
  • macroeconomic trend — 宏观经济趋势
  • macroeconomic indicator — 宏观经济指标

4. microeconomic /ˌmaɪkrəʊˌiːkəˈnɒmɪks/

n — the branch of economics concerned with individual markets, consumers, and firms
中文释义: 微观经济学。

Examples:

  • Microeconomic theory explains consumer behavior. 微观经济学理论解释消费者行为。
  • Supply and demand are central concepts in microeconomics. 供需是微观经济学的核心概念。

Common phrases:

  • microeconomic factor — 微观经济因素
  • microeconomic model — 微观经济模型
  • microeconomic behavior — 微观经济行为

5. projection /prəˈdʒɛkʃən/

n — a forecast or estimate of future events or trends; the act of projecting
中文释义: 投射；预测；规划。

Examples:

  • Economic projections suggest growth next year. 经济预测显示明年将增长。
  • The architect made a projection of the building on the wall. 建筑师在墙上投射了建筑图。

Common phrases:

  • sales projection — 销售预测
  • budget projection — 预算预测
  • future projection — 未来预测

6. disperse /dɪˈspɜːrs/

v — to scatter or spread widely; to cause to vanish
中文释义: 分散；驱散。

Examples:

  • The crowd began to disperse after the concert. 音乐会结束后人群开始散开。
  • Seeds disperse with the wind. 种子随风传播。

Common phrases:

  • disperse seeds — 播撒种子
  • disperse crowd — 驱散人群
  • disperse energy — 分散能量

7. outstanding balance /ˌaʊtˈstændɪŋ ˈbæləns/

n — the amount of money that remains unpaid
中文释义: 未结清余额。

Examples:

  • You need to pay your outstanding balance by the end of the month. 你需要在本月底前支付未结清余额。
  • The bank sent a notice about my outstanding balance. 银行寄来关于我未结清余额的通知。

Common phrases:

  • pay outstanding balance — 支付未结清余额
  • outstanding account — 未结清账户
  • monitor outstanding balance — 监控未结清余额

8. aggregation /ˌæɡrɪˈɡeɪʃən/

n — the act of collecting or bringing together items into a whole
中文释义: 聚集；集合；汇总。

Examples:

  • The aggregation of data is necessary for analysis. 数据聚合对于分析是必要的。
  • The aggregation of small companies led to a larger corporation. 小公司的合并形成了一个大公司。

Synonyms: collection, accumulation, assembly

Common phrases:

  • data aggregation — 数据汇总
  • aggregation of information — 信息聚合
  • social aggregation — 社会聚集

9. dimension /dɪˈmɛnʃən/

n — a measurable extent of something in space; an aspect or feature of a situation
中文释义: 尺寸；规模；方面。

Examples:

  • What are the dimensions of this room? 这间房间的尺寸是多少？
  • His research explores the social dimensions of education. 他的研究探讨教育的社会层面。

Synonyms: size, aspect, extent

Common phrases:

  • financial dimension — 财务维度
  • social dimension — 社会方面
  • spatial dimensions — 空间尺寸

10. via /ˈviːə/

prep — by means of a particular person, system, or route
中文释义: 通过；经由。

Examples:

  • You can send the document via email. 你可以通过电子邮件发送文件。
  • We traveled to Paris via London. 我们经过伦敦前往巴黎。

Synonyms: through, by way of, by means of

Common phrases:

  • via email — 通过电子邮件
  • via satellite — 通过卫星
  • via online platform — 经由在线平台

11. incorporate /ɪnˈkɔːpəreɪt/

v — to include or integrate something as part of a whole
中文释义: 包含；合并。

Examples:

  • The new plan incorporates all our suggestions. 新计划包含了我们所有的建议。
  • We should incorporate feedback from users into the design. 我们应该将用户反馈纳入设计中。

Synonyms: include, integrate, combine

Common phrases:

  • incorporate feedback — 纳入反馈
  • incorporate technology — 融入技术
  • incorporate ideas — 融合想法

12. embed /ɪmˈbɛd/

v — to fix something firmly into a surrounding mass or place
中文释义: 嵌入；插入。

Examples:

  • The bullet was embedded in the wall. 子弹嵌在墙里。
  • The video was embedded in the webpage. 视频被嵌入网页中。

Synonyms: implant, insert, fix

Common phrases:

  • embedded system — 嵌入式系统
  • deeply embedded — 深深植入
  • embed code — 嵌入代码
