3.堅持每天30分鐘練習英語聽力2個月

英语口语听力精华 - 对话式学习文档

日常安排与时间

1.

  • A: What time is good for you? (什么时间你方便？)
  • B: Anytime after three. (三点以后都可以。)

2.

  • A: I must get these out before noon. (我必须在中午前把这些寄出去。)
  • B: Wait outside whenever you have time is fine. (在外面等吧，你什么时候有空都行。)

外表与状态

3.

  • A: You look good in everything. (你穿什么都好看。)
  • B: I don’t care about the way I look. (我不在乎我的外表。)

4.

  • A: You’ve got dirt on your face. (你脸上有脏东西。)
  • B: Okay, I need to freshen up. (好吧，我需要梳洗一下。)

5.

  • A: How old do I sound? (我听起来多大年纪？)
  • B: Well, you look about 20. (嗯，你看起来大约20岁。)
  • A: Age is just a number. (年龄只是一个数字。)

6.

  • A: How do you keep in shape? (你是怎么保持身材的？)
  • B: I sleep a lot. (我睡很多。)
  • A: What kind of exercises do you do? (你做什么运动呢？)
  • B: I used to play a lot of tennis. These days I just work out. (我过去常打网球。最近我只是健身。)

7.

  • A: You’ve lost a lot of weight. (你瘦了很多啊。)
  • B: I was a bit overweight back then. (我那时候是有点超重。)

8.

  • A: Those shoes are on point. (这双鞋真时髦。)
  • B: Yeah, I bought them yesterday. (是的，我昨天买的。)

9.

  • A: Your hair looks nice. (你头发看起来不错。)
  • B: I just got it cut this morning. (我今天早上刚剪的。)

10.

  • A: You have incredibly thick hair. (你的头发真浓密。)
  • B: I have split ends. (我头发有分叉。)

11.

  • A: Blue is your color. (蓝色很衬你。)
  • B: Tuck your shirt in. (把你的衬衫塞进去。)

个人情况与能力

12.

  • A: What kind of person are you? (你是个什么样的人？)
  • B: I’m such an idiot. (我真是个傻瓜。)
  • A: He’s got a great personality. (他个性很好。)
  • B: That’s the way I am. (我就是这样的人。)

13.

  • A: What else is good about you? (你还有什么优点？)
  • B: I have a good memory. (我记忆力很好。)

14.

  • A: What’s he like at basketball? (他篮球打得怎么样？)
  • B: We need another player. Um, don’t ask him to play. He’s more of a soccer player. (我们还需要个队员。嗯，别叫他打。他更像是个踢足球的。)

15.

  • A: Is he any good at math? (他数学好吗？)
  • B: You’ll have to ask him. (你得自己去问他。)

16.

  • A: Can you change a tire on a car? (你会换车胎吗？)
  • B: Yeah, I think I can do that. Um, actually I’ve never driven a car. I’ll just call a taxi. (是的，我想我会。嗯，其实我从没开过车。我还是叫出租车吧。)
  • A: Well, what about the car keys? (那车钥匙呢？)
  • B: Drop them off at the table. (放桌上就行。)

17.

  • A: Where did you put it? (你把它放哪儿了？)
  • B: Right on the shelf. (就在架子上。)

18.

  • A: Would you like another one? (你想再来一个吗？)
  • B: I couldn’t possibly eat anymore. (我实在吃不下了。)
  • A: What are you afraid of? (你怕什么？)
  • B: I’ve been eating too much. (我吃得太多了。)
  • A: Don’t forget about the dishes. (别忘了洗碗。)
  • B: I’ll be sure to clean up after dinner. (我保证饭后会收拾干净。)

19.

  • A: What does your mom do? (你妈妈是做什么的？)
  • B: She works at a diner. (她在小餐馆工作。)
  • A: I’m not a picky eater. (我不挑食。)
  • B: I don’t like mushrooms overall. (我总的来说不喜欢蘑菇。)

20.

  • A: What are your feelings about him? (你觉得他怎么样？)
  • B: He can be such a jerk sometimes. (他有时候真是个混蛋。)
  • A: We spoke on the phone the other day. (我们前几天通过电话。)
  • B: Just put him on the phone. (让他接电话。)

21.

  • A: Why don’t you rest a little? (你为什么不休息一下？)
  • B: I want a day off. (我想放一天假。)

22.

  • A: Do you have any hobbies? (你有什么爱好吗？)
  • B: My main interest is jazz. (我主要对爵士乐感兴趣。)

23.

  • A: What is it that you’re looking for? (你在寻找什么？)
  • B: I’ve always wanted to be a singer. (我一直想当个歌手。)

24.

  • A: I’m not sure how the others feel. (我不确定其他人怎么想。)
  • B: I’ll find out soon enough. (我很快就会知道的。)

25.

  • A: When is the application due? (申请什么时候截止？)
  • B: It’s due next Friday. (下周五截止。)
  • A: I don’t know how to fill it out. Could you help me out with it? (我不知道怎么填。你能帮帮我吗？)
  • B: I’ve always been lazy. (我一直很懒。)
  • A: Don’t be so hard on yourself. (别对自己太苛刻了。)

观点与反应

26.

  • A: It’s a little scary. (有点吓人。)
  • B: I think you can handle it. (我觉得你能应付。)

27.

  • A: This is just not my kind of thing. (这真不是我的菜。)
  • B: Keep your mind open. (保持开放的心态。)

28.

  • A: I’ve watched every episode. It’s one of my favorites. (每一集我都看了。这是我最喜欢的之一。)
  • B: How was your reaction? (你觉得怎么样？)
  • A: I thought it was the best movie ever. (我觉得这是有史以来最好的电影。)
  • B: The movie is starting to drag on. (电影开始变得拖沓了。)
  • A: That’s just what I was thinking. (我也是这么想的。)
  • B: Do you find it fun? (你觉得好玩吗？)
  • A: I’m not into action movies. (我不喜欢动作片。)

29.

  • A: She said it’s not good enough. (她说这不够好。)
  • B: She’s got a point. (她说得有道理。)

30.

  • A: Could you stay a little later? (你能晚点走吗？)
  • B: You know I don’t have any plans after work anyway. (你知道我反正下班后也没安排。)
  • A: That’s a great idea. It’ll be a lot of fun. I’ve got tickets already. (好主意。一定会很有趣。我已经有票了。)

31.

  • A: Where should I meet you? (我在哪儿见你？)
  • B: I’ll meet you halfway. (我们找个中间点见吧。)
  • A: The bus comes every half hour. (公交车每半小时一班。)
  • B: Are you sure about it? (你确定吗？)
  • A: We were late and you got angry. (我们迟到了，你当时很生气。)
  • B: I’ll make sure to leave on time. (我会确保准时出门的。)

冲突与解决

32.

  • A: She screwed up everything. (她把一切都搞砸了。)
  • B: She was just trying to help. (她只是想帮忙。)

33.

  • A: He never lets me go out. (他从不让我出门。)
  • B: He wants to keep you out of trouble. (他是想让你远离麻烦。)

34.

  • A: We got into a fight today. (我们今天吵架了。)
  • B: I don’t want any trouble. (我不想惹麻烦。)
  • A: Where were you when it happened? (事发时你在哪儿？)
  • B: I was out in the street. (我在外面的街上。)

35.

  • A: Did I wake you up? (我吵醒你了吗？)
  • B: I’m a light sleeper. (我睡觉很轻。)

36.

  • A: I’m sorry about last night. (昨晚的事很抱歉。)
  • B: I think I was probably overreacting. (我想我可能反应过激了。)
  • A: She’s always had a problem with me. (她一直对我不满。)
  • B: You two need to talk it out. (你们俩需要好好谈谈。)

提议与请求

37.

  • A: Do you mind if I sit here? (你介意我坐这儿吗？)
  • B: I don’t see why not. (我看没什么不行的。)

38.

  • A: It’ll be nice in the park today. (今天公园里应该很不错。)
  • B: I think I’ll go for a walk. (我想我会去散个步。)

39.

  • A: I’ve had enough of that food. I want something else. (那种食物我吃够了。我想要点别的。)
  • B: You can’t go around looking like that. (你不能就这个样子到处走。)
  • A: Dad, I’m 17. I can wear what I want. (爸，我17岁了。我想穿什么就穿什么。)

40.

  • A: I got caught up at work. (我被工作耽搁了。)
  • B: I’ve been waiting for 20 minutes. (我已经等了20分钟了。)

41.

  • A: Watch where you’re going. (看着点路。)
  • B: I’m in a terrible rush. (我急着赶时间。)
  • A: I’m trying to get some work done. (我正想干点活呢。)
  • B: I didn’t realize it bothered you. (我没意识到打扰到你了。)

42.

  • A: Do you mind if I took your picture? (你介意我给你拍张照吗？)
  • B: Well, that’ll be a dollar. (嗯，那要一美元。)

43.

  • A: What would you like for Christmas? (你想要什么圣诞礼物？)
  • B: I’m hoping for a brand new bike. (我想要一辆全新的自行车。)

44.

  • A: You’re not alone anymore. (你不再是孤单一人了。)
  • B: I’ll be there in a minute. (我马上就到。)

45.

  • A: Can I borrow your pen? (能借你的笔吗？)
  • B: I’m afraid mine has just run out. (恐怕我的刚好没水了。)

46.

  • A: It’s a little hot in here. (这里有点热。)
  • B: Let me turn on the air conditioning. (让我把空调打开。)

47.

  • A: Let’s wait a bit longer. (我们再等一会儿吧。)
  • B: I don’t think he’s gonna show up. (我觉得他不会来了。)
  • A: Well, that’s surprising. I didn’t expect him to show up either. (哦，真意外。我也没指望他会来。)

48.

  • A: I’m afraid the cost will be 200. (恐怕费用是200。)
  • B: I thought you said it would be 50. (我记得你说的是50。)

49.

  • A: That guy really irritates me. (那家伙真让我恼火。)
  • B: They’re all like that. (他们都一个样。)

50.

  • A: I don’t think I can make it. (我觉得我做不到/赶不上了。)
  • B: You’re doing fine. (你做得很好。)

51.

  • A: I got my driver’s license today, mom. (妈，我今天拿到驾照了。)
  • B: I’m behind you all the way. (我完全支持你。)

52.

  • A: My friend’s car got stolen. (我朋友的车被偷了。)
  • B: When did it happen? (什么时候发生的？)

53.

  • A: Sorry about the mess. (不好意思，这里很乱。)
  • B: No problem, you should see my apartment. (没事，你真该看看我的公寓。)

54.

  • A: My internet is having issues right now. It’s lagging a little bit. (我的网络现在有问题。有点卡。)
  • B: I’ll let you go. (那我先不打扰你了。)
  • A: I gotta go back to work. (我得回去工作了。)

55.

  • A: Can you shut the door please? (请问能关下门吗？)
  • B: We’re just gonna leave it open. (我们就开着吧。)

56.

  • A: I locked myself out of my house. I was hoping you could help me. (我把自己锁在门外了。希望你能帮帮我。)
  • B: I’m sorry you feel that way. I think we can move on. (我为你这么想感到遗憾。我想我们可以翻篇了。)

57.

  • A: I’d like to take you to lunch. (我想请你吃午饭。)
  • B: Why don’t we all get dinner? (我们何不一起去吃晚饭呢？)
  • A: Do you want to go to the movies tonight? (今晚想去看电影吗？)
  • B: I’d rather just stay home. (我宁愿呆在家里。)
  • A: You need to loosen up and have fun. I’m gonna put some music on. (你需要放松找点乐子。我来放点音乐。)

58.

  • A: Can I borrow the car tonight? (今晚能借车用吗？)
  • B: Yeah, if you take out the trash first. (可以，如果你先把垃圾倒了。)

59.

  • A: I need your advice. (我需要你的建议。)
  • B: You don’t usually come to me for advice. (你通常可不来找我拿主意。)

60.

  • A: Are you all right taking the bus home? (你坐公交回家行吗？)
  • B: I can drive you home, you know. (我可以开车送你回家的，你知道。)
  • A: I just came from work. I know how busy you are. (我刚下班。我知道你有多忙。)

61.

  • A: Are you nervous about your trip? (你对旅行感到紧张吗？)
  • B: I’ve never been this far away from home before. (我以前从没离家这么远过。)

62.

  • A: I couldn’t have done it without you. (没有你我不可能完成。)
  • B: I enjoyed working on it with you. (我很享受和你一起完成它。)
  • A: Thank you for all of your efforts. (感谢你所有的努力。)
  • B: I’m extremely grateful to you. (我非常感激你。)

63.

  • A: Tomorrow everybody has the day off. (明天大家都放假。)
  • B: That sounds like fun. (听起来很有趣。)
