1. remediation /rɪˌmiːdiˈeɪʃən/

n — the action of correcting a problem or improving a situation
中文释义: 补救；改进措施；补习

Examples:

  • There’s going to have to be some remediation to fix the issue.
  • The school offered remediation for students who failed the exam.

Common Phrases:

  • remediation plan — 补救计划
  • academic remediation — 学术补习

Word Root:

  • Root: remed- (Latin: “to cure, remedy”) → 与治疗、补救相关
  • Suffix: -ation 表示名词
  • Formation: remed + -ation → remediation
  • Related Words:
    • remedy — 补救，治疗
    • remedial — 补救的，矫正的
