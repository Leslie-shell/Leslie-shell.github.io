1. despondently /dɪˈspɒndəntli/

adv — in a way that shows you are unhappy because you have no hope or enthusiasm
中文释义：沮丧地；情绪低落地（牛津）

Examples:

  • He replied despondently after hearing the bad news.
  • She sat despondently by the window.

Word Root:

  • Root: spond (Latin respondere: “to promise, answer”)
  • Related Words:
    • despondent — 沮丧的
    • respond — 回答

2. dental /ˈdentəl/

adj — relating to the teeth
中文释义：牙齿的；牙科的（牛津）

Examples:

  • She has a dental appointment tomorrow.
  • Dental care is important for overall health.

Word Root:

  • Root: dent (Latin: “tooth”)
  • Related Words:
    • dentist — 牙医
    • dentistry — 牙科学

3. trifles /ˈtraɪflz/

n — unimportant or small matters
中文释义：琐事；小事（牛津）

Examples:

  • He was always worried about trifles.
  • Don’t waste time on trifles when the deadline is near.

Word Root:

  • Origin: Old French trufle — joke, nonsense
  • Related Words:
    • trifle — 小事；琐事
    • trifling — 微不足道的

4. sacrifice /ˈsækrɪfaɪs/

v — to give up something important or valuable to get something else
中文释义：牺牲；放弃（牛津）

Examples:

  • Parents often sacrifice their own needs for their children.
  • He sacrificed sleep to finish the project.

Word Root:

  • Root: sacr (Latin: “holy, sacred”)
  • Related Words:
    • sacred — 神圣的
    • sacrificial — 牺牲的

5. gleamed /ɡliːmd/

v — to shine softly; to show a small sign of emotion
中文释义：闪烁；（情感）闪现（牛津）

Examples:

  • Her eyes gleamed with excitement.
  • The knife gleamed under the light.

Word Root:

  • Origin: Old English glǣm — brightness, radiance
  • Related Words:
    • gleam — 微光；闪光

6. astonishing /əˈstɒnɪʃɪŋ/

adj — very surprising; difficult to believe
中文释义：令人惊讶的；惊人的（牛津）

Examples:

  • The long-distance runner made astonishing progress.
  • It is astonishing how fast technology changes.

Word Root:

  • Root: aston- from “to stun, to strike with sudden surprise”
  • Related Words:
    • astonish — 使惊讶
    • astonishment — 惊讶

7. obligations /ˌɒblɪˈɡeɪʃənz/

n — things you must do because of law, duty, or promise
中文：义务；责任

Examples:

  • Parents have legal obligations to care for their children.
  • He felt obligated to attend the meeting.

Synonyms: responsibilities, duties, commitments

Word Root:

  • ob-（toward） + lig（bind 绑）
    → 原义：被捆绑 → “义务、责任”
  • Related: oblige, obligatory, obligation
