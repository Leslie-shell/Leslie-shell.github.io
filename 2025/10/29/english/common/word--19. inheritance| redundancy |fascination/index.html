1. inheritance /ɪnˈherɪtəns/

n — money, property, or qualities passed down from someone after they die
中文释义: 遗产；继承物。

Examples:

  • She received a large inheritance from her grandmother.
  • Inheritance laws vary from country to country.

Common Phrases:

  • inheritance tax — 遗产税
  • legal inheritance — 法定继承
  • inheritance rights — 继承权

Synonyms:

  • legacy
  • bequest
  • heritage

Word Root:

  • Root: her- (Latin: “to inherit”) → 与继承相关
  • Suffix: -ance 表示名词
  • Formation: inherit + -ance → inheritance
  • Related Words:
    • inherit — 继承
    • heritable — 可继承的
    • inheritor — 继承人

2. redundancy /rɪˈdʌndənsi/

n — the state of being no longer needed, superfluous, or repetitive
中文释义: 多余；冗余；重复。

Examples:

  • The company announced redundancies due to restructuring.
  • Avoid redundancy in your essay to make it concise.

Common Phrases:

  • job redundancy — 裁员
  • redundancy in writing — 写作冗余
  • system redundancy — 系统冗余

Synonyms:

  • surplus
  • repetition
  • excess

Word Root:

  • Root: dun- (Latin: “to lead, to push”) → 与重复或多余相关
  • Prefix: re- 表示“再次、重复”
  • Suffix: -ancy 表示名词
  • Formation: re + dund + -ancy → redundancy
  • Related Words:
    • redundant — 多余的
    • redundantly — 冗余地
    • redundancy plan — 冗余计划

3. fascination /ˌfæsɪˈneɪʃən/

n — a strong interest or attraction for someone or something
中文释义: 着迷；极大兴趣。

Examples:

  • He has a fascination with ancient history.
  • The child watched the magician’s tricks with fascination.

Common Phrases:

  • intense fascination — 强烈兴趣
  • fascination with — 对……着迷
  • scientific fascination — 科学兴趣

Synonyms:

  • captivation
  • attraction
  • enchantment

Word Root:

  • Root: fascin- (Latin: “to enchant, to bewitch”) → 与吸引、迷惑相关
  • Suffix: -ation 表示名词
  • Formation: fascin + -ation → fascination
  • Related Words:
    • fascinate — 使着迷
    • fascinating — 迷人的
    • fascination for — 对……的兴趣
