controversial /ˌkɒntrəˈvɜːrʃəl/

adjective — causing disagreement or discussion

中文释义: 有争议的；引起争论的。

Examples:

  • The new policy is highly controversial among politicians.
  • His speech on climate change was controversial.

Synonyms

  • disputed, debatable, contentious

Common Phrases

  • controversial issue — 有争议的问题
    • The death penalty remains a controversial issue worldwide.
  • controversial topic — 有争议的话题
    • Smoking in public places is a controversial topic.

organized /ˈɔːrɡənaɪzd/

adjective — arranged or structured in a systematic way

中文释义: 有组织的；安排有序的。

Examples:

  • She kept her files neatly organized.
  • The event was well organized and ran smoothly.

Synonyms

  • systematic, orderly, structured

Common Phrases

  • well organized — 组织良好的
    • A well-organized schedule helps reduce stress.
  • organized effort — 有组织的努力
    • The organized effort of volunteers cleaned the park.

thematic /θɪˈmætɪk/

adjective — relating to a particular subject or theme

中文释义: 主题的；有关主题的。

Examples:

  • The report follows a thematic structure.
  • The museum organized a thematic exhibition on ancient civilizations.

Synonyms

  • topical, subject-based, focused

Common Phrases

  • thematic approach — 主题方法
    • The teacher used a thematic approach to teaching literature.
  • thematic analysis — 主题分析
    • Researchers conducted a thematic analysis of the interviews.
