背单词Mission | Disseminate |Assignment

1. mission /ˈmɪʃən/

n — an important job that someone is given to do, especially when it is difficult or dangerous. (Oxford)
中文释义: 使命；任务。

Examples:

  • Their mission is to provide clean water to the village.
  • The astronauts completed their mission successfully.

Common Phrases:

  • secret mission — 秘密任务
  • life mission — 人生使命
  • military mission — 军事任务

Synonyms:

  • task
  • assignment
  • duty

Word Root:

  • Root: miss- (Latin: “to send”) → 与派遣、任务相关
  • Suffix: -ion 表示名词
  • Formation: miss + -ion → mission
  • Related Words:
    • missionary — 传教士
    • remission — 宽恕，减免

2. disseminate /dɪˈsemɪneɪt/

v — to spread information, knowledge, ideas, etc. so that they reach many people. (Oxford)
中文释义: 传播；散布。

Examples:

  • The internet helps to disseminate information quickly.
  • The organization disseminates educational materials to schools.

Common Phrases:

  • disseminate knowledge — 传播知识
  • disseminate information — 散布信息
  • widely disseminated — 广泛传播

Synonyms:

  • spread
  • circulate
  • distribute

Word Root:

  • Prefix: dis- (Latin: “apart”) → 分开，散开
  • Root: semin- (Latin: “seed”) → 与播种、传播相关
  • Suffix: -ate 表示动词
  • Formation: dis + semin + -ate → disseminate
  • Related Words:
    • dissemination — 传播，散布
    • seminate — 播种

3. assignment /əˈsaɪnmənt/

n — a task or piece of work that is given to someone as part of their job or studies. (Oxford)
中文释义: 作业；任务；分配。

Examples:

  • The teacher gave us a challenging assignment.
  • He completed the assignment ahead of schedule.

Common Phrases:

  • homework assignment — 家庭作业
  • project assignment — 项目任务
  • assignment deadline — 作业截止日期

Synonyms:

  • task
  • duty
  • job

Word Root:

  • Root: sign- (Latin: “mark, sign”) → 与标记、指派相关
  • Suffix: -ment 表示名词
  • Formation: assign + -ment → assignment
  • Related Words:
    • assign — 分配，指派
    • reassignment — 重新分配
