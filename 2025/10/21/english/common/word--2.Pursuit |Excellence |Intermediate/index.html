pursuit /pərˈsuːt/

noun — the action of trying to achieve or obtain something

中文释义: 追求；追逐。

Examples:

  • His pursuit of knowledge never ends.
  • The police were in pursuit of the suspect.

Synonyms: quest, chase, striving

Common Phrases

  • in pursuit of — 追求；寻求
    • She traveled the world in pursuit of adventure.
  • pursuit of excellence — 追求卓越
    • Continuous learning is essential in the pursuit of excellence.

excellence /ˈɛksələns/

noun — the quality of being outstanding or extremely good

中文释义: 卓越；杰出。

Examples:

  • The school is known for academic excellence.
  • She strives for excellence in all her work.

Synonyms: superiority, distinction, brilliance

Common Phrases

  • pursuit of excellence — 追求卓越
    • Employees are encouraged to engage in the pursuit of excellence.
  • academic excellence — 学术卓越
    • Scholarships are awarded for academic excellence.
  • center of excellence — 卓越中心
    • The hospital is a center of excellence for heart surgery.

intermediate /ˌɪntərˈmiːdiət/

adjective — at a level between beginner and advanced

中文释义: 中级的；中等水平的。

Examples:

  • He is taking an intermediate English course.
  • This recipe is suitable for cooks of intermediate skill.

Synonyms: middle-level, moderate, transitional

Common Phrases

  • intermediate level — 中级水平
    • Students at an intermediate level can participate in this workshop.
  • intermediate course — 中级课程
    • The school offers beginner, intermediate, and advanced courses.
  • intermediate skills — 中级技能
    • Developing intermediate skills is key to progressing further.
