5.Logistics |Coordination |Finance

1. logistics /ləˈdʒɪstɪks/

n — the detailed organization and implementation of a complex operation
中文释义: 物流；后勤管理。

Examples:

  • The company improved its logistics to speed up delivery.
  • Event logistics require careful planning and coordination.

Common Phrases:

  • supply chain logistics — 供应链物流
  • logistics management — 物流管理
  • logistics support — 后勤支持

Synonyms:

  • supply management
  • operations
  • coordination

Word Root:

  • Root: log- (Greek: “study, word, reason”) → 与计算、安排和组织相关
  • Formation: logistic + -s → logistics
  • Related Words:
    • logistician — 物流专家
    • logistics system — 物流系统

2. coordination /kɔːˌɔːrdɪˈneɪʃən/

n — the organization of people or things to work together effectively
中文释义: 协调；配合。

Examples:

  • Coordination between departments is crucial for success.
  • They worked in close coordination to complete the project.

Common Phrases:

  • in coordination with — 与……协调
  • coordination meeting — 协调会议
  • team coordination — 团队协调

Synonyms:

  • organization
  • collaboration
  • integration

Word Root:

  • Root: ordin- (Latin: “order, arrange”) → 与安排、秩序相关
  • Suffix: -ation 表示名词
  • Formation: coordinate + -ion → coordination
  • Related Words:
    • coordinate — 协调
    • coordinator — 协调员
    • coordinate system — 坐标系统

3. finance /ˈfaɪnæns/

n — the management of money, banking, investments, and credit
中文释义: 财务；金融。

Examples:

  • The finance department handles all company budgets.
  • She studied finance at university.

Common Phrases:

  • personal finance — 个人理财
  • corporate finance — 企业财务
  • public finance — 公共财政

Synonyms:

  • money management
  • banking
  • accounting

Word Root:

  • Root: fin- (Latin: “end, limit”) → 与钱财、支付有关
  • Formation: finance ← fin + -ance
  • Related Words:
    • financial — 财务的
    • financier — 金融家
    • refinance — 重新融资
