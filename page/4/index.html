    14. impetuous /ɪmˈpetʃuəs/

    adj — acting or done quickly and without thought or care
    中文释义: 冲动的；鲁莽的

    Examples:

    • His impetuous behavior often gets him into trouble.
    • She made an impetuous decision to quit her job.

    Common Phrases:

    • impetuous decision — 冲动的决定
    • impetuous temperament — 鲁莽的性格

    Synonyms:

    • impulsive
    • rash
    • hasty

    Word Root:

    • Root: impet- (Latin: “attack, force”) → 与冲动、猛力相关
    • Suffix: -uous 表形容词
    • Formation: impet + -uous → impetuous
    • Related Words:
      • impetus — 推动力
      • impetuosity — 冲动性
