English Word in use （Basic Greetings） --elementray

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， WordInUse 阅读次数：
本文字数： 3.6k 阅读时长 ≈ 3 分钟

1. Basic Greetings (基本问候)

  1. Hello! /həˈloʊ/ 你好！
    • Hello! How are you today?
  2. Hi! /haɪ/ 嗨！
    • Hi! Nice to see you!
  3. Good morning! /ɡʊd ˈmɔːrnɪŋ/ 早上好！
    • Good morning! Did you sleep well?
  4. Good afternoon! /ɡʊd ˌæftərˈnuːn/ 下午好！
    • Good afternoon! It’s sunny today.
  5. Good evening! /ɡʊd ˈiːvnɪŋ/ 晚上好！
    • Good evening! Are you here for dinner?
  6. Good night! /ɡʊd naɪt/ 晚安！
    • Good night! Sweet dreams!
  7. Hey! /heɪ/ 嘿！（非正式）
    • Hey! Wait for me!

2. Asking About Someone (询问近况)

  1. How are you? /haʊ ɑːr ju/ 你好吗？
    • How are you? I haven’t seen you in ages!
  2. How’s it going? /haʊz ɪt ˈɡoʊɪŋ/ 最近怎么样？（非正式）
    • Hey Mark! How’s it going?
  3. How are things? /haʊ ɑːr θɪŋz/ 情况如何？
    • How are things at work?
  4. What’s up? /wʌts ʌp/ 怎么了？（非常非正式）
    • What’s up? You look tired.
  5. How have you been? /haʊ hæv ju bɪn/ 你最近好吗？
    • Long time no see! How have you been?

3. Responding to Greetings (回应问候)

  1. I’m fine, thanks. /aɪm faɪn θæŋks/ 我很好，谢谢。
    • “How are you?” “I’m fine, thanks!”
  2. Not bad. /nɒt bæd/ 还不错。
    • “How’s work?” “Not bad!”
  3. Great! /ɡreɪt/ 很棒！
    • “How are you?” “Great! I just got a new job!”
  4. Could be better. /kʊd bi ˈbetər/ 还行吧（不太理想）。
    • “How are you feeling?” “Could be better—I have a cold.”
  5. Same as usual. /seɪm əz ˈjuːʒuəl/ 老样子。
    • “How’s life?” “Same as usual.”

4. Introductions (介绍)

  1. My name is… /maɪ neɪm ɪz/ 我的名字是…
    • My name is Sarah. Nice to meet you!
  2. What’s your name? /wʌts jɔːr neɪm/ 你叫什么名字？
    • Hi! What’s your name?
  3. This is… /ðɪs ɪz/ 这是…（介绍他人）
    • This is my friend, Tom.
  4. Nice to meet you! /naɪs tə miːt ju/ 很高兴认识你！
    • “I’m Alex.” “Nice to meet you, Alex!”
  5. Pleased to meet you. /pliːzd tə miːt ju/ 幸会。（正式）
    • “I’m Dr. Lee.” “Pleased to meet you.”

5. Saying Goodbye (道别)

  1. Goodbye! /ɡʊdˈbaɪ/ 再见！
    • Goodbye! See you next week!
  2. Bye! /baɪ/ 拜拜！（非正式）
    • Bye! Take care!
  3. See you later! /siː ju ˈleɪtər/ 待会儿见！
    • See you later at the café!
  4. See you soon! /siː ju suːn/ 很快见！
    • See you soon! Don’t be late!
  5. Take care! /teɪk keər/ 保重！
    • Bye, Mom! Take care!
  6. Have a nice day! /hæv ə naɪs deɪ/ 祝你今天愉快！
    • Thanks! Have a nice day too!

English Word in use （Common Illnesses & Symptoms） --elementray

发表于 2025-11-29 分类于 English ， Vocabulary ， WordInUse 阅读次数：
本文字数： 3.3k 阅读时长 ≈ 3 分钟

1. Common Illnesses & Symptoms (常见疾病与症状)

  1. cold /koʊld/ 感冒
    • I have a cold, so I’m sneezing a lot.
  2. flu /fluː/ 流感
    • She missed work because she had the flu.
  3. fever /ˈfiːvər/ 发烧
    • His forehead is hot—he must have a fever.
  4. cough /kɒf/ 咳嗽
    • My cough keeps me awake at night.
  5. sore throat /sɔːr θroʊt/ 喉咙痛
    • Drink tea with honey for a sore throat.
  6. headache /ˈhedeɪk/ 头痛
    • Stress gives me terrible headaches.
  7. stomachache /ˈstʌməkˌeɪk/ 胃痛
    • He ate too much and got a stomachache.
  8. toothache /ˈtuːθeɪk/ 牙痛
    • I need to see the dentist for this toothache.
  9. rash /ræʃ/ 皮疹
    • The baby has a red rash on her arms.
  10. allergy /ˈælərdʒi/ 过敏
    • My allergy to cats makes me sneeze.

2. Body Problems (身体不适)

  1. pain /peɪn/ 疼痛
    • There’s a sharp pain in my back.
  2. ache /eɪk/ （持续）疼痛
    • My legs ache after running.
  3. bruise /bruːz/ 淤青
    • She got a bruise when she fell.
  4. cut /kʌt/ 割伤
    • Wash the cut with soap and water.
  5. burn /bɜːrn/ 烧伤
    • He got a burn from hot oil.
  6. bleed /bliːd/ 流血
    • Her knee started to bleed after the fall.
  7. swollen /ˈswoʊlən/ 肿胀的
    • My ankle is swollen after twisting it.
  8. itchy /ˈɪtʃi/ 发痒的
    • Mosquito bites make my skin itchy.
  9. dizzy /ˈdɪzi/ 头晕的
    • Stand up slowly or you’ll feel dizzy.
  10. nauseous /ˈnɔːʃəs/ 恶心的
    • The boat ride made me feel nauseous.

3. Health Actions (健康措施)

  1. rest /rest/ 休息
    • You should rest when you’re sick.
  2. sleep /sliːp/ 睡觉
    • Getting enough sleep helps your immune system.
  3. exercise /ˈeksərsaɪz/ 锻炼
    • Regular exercise keeps your heart healthy.
  4. diet /ˈdaɪət/ 饮食
    • A balanced diet includes fruits and vegetables.
  5. medicine /ˈmedɪsn/ 药物
    • Take this medicine twice a day.
  6. pill /pɪl/ 药片
    • She swallowed a pill for her headache.
  7. bandage /ˈbændɪdʒ/ 绷带
    • Put a bandage on the cut to protect it.
  8. vaccine /ˈvæksiːn/ 疫苗
    • The flu vaccine prevents serious illness.
  9. appointment /əˈpɔɪntmənt/ 预约
    • I made an appointment with the doctor.
  10. prescription /prɪˈskrɪpʃn/ 处方
    • You need a prescription for this antibiotic.

4. Doctor & Hospital (医生与医院)

  1. doctor (Dr.) /ˈdɒktər/ 医生
    • The doctor listened to my heartbeat.
  2. nurse /nɜːrs/ 护士
    • The nurse checked my blood pressure.
  3. hospital /ˈhɒspɪtl/ 医院
    • He was rushed to the hospital after the accident.
  4. clinic /ˈklɪnɪk/ 诊所
    • There’s a free clinic near our school.
  5. emergency /ɪˈmɜːrdʒənsi/ 急诊
    • Call 911 in an emergency!
  6. X-ray /ˈeks reɪ/ X光
    • The X-ray showed a broken bone.
  7. surgery /ˈsɜːrdʒəri/ 手术
    • She had surgery on her knee yesterday.
  8. recover /rɪˈkʌvər/ 康复
    • It takes two weeks to recover from the flu.
  9. health /helθ/ 健康
    • Smoking is bad for your health.
  10. ill /ɪl/ 生病的
    • She felt too ill to go to work.

5. Advice & Phrases (建议与短语)

  1. Stay hydrated! /steɪ ˈhaɪdreɪtɪd/ 多喝水！
    • When you have a fever, stay hydrated!
  2. Get well soon! /ɡet wel suːn/ 早日康复！
    • I sent her a card saying “Get well soon!”
  3. How are you feeling? /haʊ ɑːr ju ˈfiːlɪŋ/ 你感觉怎么样？
    • “How are you feeling?” “Much better, thanks!”
  4. I don’t feel well. /aɪ doʊnt fiːl wel/ 我不舒服。
    • Mom, I don’t feel well—my stomach hurts.
  5. Call a doctor. /kɔːl ə ˈdɒktər/ 叫医生。
    • If the pain worsens, call a doctor.
  6. Take your temperature. /teɪk jɔːr ˈtemprətʃər/ 量体温。
    • Use this thermometer to take your temperature.
  7. Cover your mouth when you cough. /ˈkʌvər jɔːr maʊθ wen ju kɒf/ 咳嗽时捂住嘴。
    • It’s polite to cover your mouth when you cough.
  8. Wash your hands. /wɒʃ jɔːr hændz/ 洗手。
    • Wash your hands before eating.
  9. Get some fresh air. /ɡet sʌm freʃ er/ 呼吸新鲜空气。
    • Go outside and get some fresh air—it’ll help!
  10. Avoid sugary drinks. /əˈvɔɪd ˈʃʊɡəri drɪŋks/ 避免含糖饮料。
    • Avoid sugary drinks when you’re sick.
